The loud recall is NOT 1.0 on every cell and the reason is a deliberate trade in the decision table. The rules predict a loud failure only from a physical bound violation, which maps to a documented SGP4 error code. An element set whose characters have shifted so far that a field will not parse is classified silently wrong instead, with an infinite equation (15) score, because the propagator very often reads such a line without complaint and returns a confident wrong position. The misses below are the records where the propagator did refuse it after all.

6. THE STRUCTURAL LIMIT OF THE CROSS-SOURCE SCREEN, MEASURED
==============================================================================
The corpus contains two constructions of the same observable situation. In the cross_source_divergence family the candidate is the perturbed source and the other source is clean, so the measured consequence is real. In the uncorrupted family with a second source attached the candidate is the clean one and the other source is perturbed, so the measured consequence is zero. The two are indistinguishable from the two element sets alone, because equation (12) is symmetric. No screen can be right on both and the measured cost is below.
