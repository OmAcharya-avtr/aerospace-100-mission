MEASUREMENT MODEL. The platform rail is VDD_IN, measured by the on-board INA3221 as bus voltage (mV) times shunt current (mA); P(t) = V(t)*I(t). Energy over a window is the TRAPEZOIDAL integral of P(t) over the sampler's CLOCK_MONOTONIC timestamps. A cycle is 2.0 s: the pipeline runs back-to-back inferences for duty*2.0 s and sleeps the rest; a run is 38 cycles. An inference is one pass over one frame, one row of the decisions CSV. A USEFUL DECISION is an inference that passes the gate -- a candidate exists, its box does not touch the border, its area is within the declared bounds, and its score is at least tau -- so the acceptance rate is counted from the committed CSV and never asserted. ENERGY PER USEFUL DECISION is the integral of P(t) over the WHOLE run, sleep windows included, divided by the number of accepted decisions in that run.

closed-form relative tolerance: 1e-09
The power column is written with %.3f, exactly as data/campaign.py writes it, so the exact closed form and the file cannot agree better than about 1e-11 relative.
