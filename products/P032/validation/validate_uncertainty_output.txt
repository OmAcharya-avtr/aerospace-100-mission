Uncertainty analysis
========================================================================================

Part 1 -- along-track propagation error to window-edge shift
  method: perturb the mean anomaly by dM = ds / a (circular-orbit arc
          length, exact for a circular orbit; Vallado 2013 Ch. 2) and
          recompute the ground windows. 6 h horizon, 30 s scan.
  the along-track error is a user input, not a result of this package.
     ds [km]     dM [rad]  n win   max |dt| [s]   mean dt_open [s]   mean d_dur [s]
  ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
        0.10    1.443e-05      3         0.0293            -0.0098          -0.0000
        1.00    1.443e-04      3         0.1465            -0.0879          -0.0391
        5.00    7.217e-04      3         0.7031            -0.4590          -0.2246
       20.00    2.887e-03      3         2.7832            -1.8359          -0.9082
  edge shift per km of along-track error: 0.2930, 0.1465, 0.1406, 0.1392 s/km
  the slope is near the reciprocal of the ground-track speed, which
  is the expected first-order behaviour and is reported, not asserted.
  the smallest perturbation sits at the 0.05 s default bisection
  tolerance floor, which is why its apparent slope is larger; the
  three larger perturbations are the ones to read.

Part 3 -- bisection refinement tolerance
     tol [s]  n win   max |dt_open| [s]   max |dt_close| [s]
  ----------------------------------------------------------
       1e-06      8            0.000000             0.000000
       1e-03      8            0.000409             0.000438
       5e-02      8            0.005542             0.005228
       1e+00      8            0.453885             0.455467
       1e+01      8            3.555318             3.562223
  edge error is bounded by the tolerance and is independent of the
  grid step, because bisection re-propagates rather than interpolating.
