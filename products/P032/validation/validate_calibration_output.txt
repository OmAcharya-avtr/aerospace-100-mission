  equal-width bins: the residual is the within-bin component of
    Stephenson, Coelho & Jolliffe 2008 -- real, not rounding error.
  distinct-value bins: the three-term identity is exact and the
    residual is floating-point only. This is the exactness check.
  Note the degeneracy it exposes: for a continuous predictor every
    distinct-value bin holds one row, so REL collapses onto the
    Brier score and RES onto UNC. The distinct-value decomposition
    is an identity check, NOT a calibration measurement; the
    equal-width rows are the ones to read for calibration.
