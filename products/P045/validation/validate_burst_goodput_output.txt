Headline, stated as a practitioner would need it:
  at a 5 per cent frame error rate on an N = 60 link, the
  independent-error go-back-N formula is wrong by -74.1 per cent
  once errors arrive in bursts of 100 frames, and the
  selective-repeat formula at the textbook window is wrong by
  +62.5 per cent at bursts of 2 frames.
  Stop-and-wait is wrong by at most +0.52 per cent,
  as predicted, because its throughput is linear in the marginal rate.
