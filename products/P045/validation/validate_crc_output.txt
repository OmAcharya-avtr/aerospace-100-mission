Notes. The error patterns are 1 to 8 distinct bit flips over a
544-bit frame (CRC-32 case).  A first pass of this check sampled flip
positions with replacement, which silently injected no-error frames
and produced 7 spurious misses in 20000 for CRC-32; the sampling is
now without replacement and that artefact is gone.  These rates are
for a pattern a CRC is good at.  They are not a residual-undetected-
error model for a real channel, and this package does not provide one.
