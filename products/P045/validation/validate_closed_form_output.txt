Reading: at W = N, which is one bandwidth-delay product plus one
frame and is the textbook recommendation, selective repeat is
already well short of its ideal throughput, and the shortfall grows
with the frame error rate. The window needed to come within 1 per
cent of 1-p is several times N, not N. That multiple is the number
the textbook rule hides, and it is the reason this repository
simulates the state machine instead of evaluating the formula.
