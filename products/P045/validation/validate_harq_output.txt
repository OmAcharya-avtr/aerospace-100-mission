Reading: every link preset here is between four and six orders of
magnitude past the crossover, so for a single HARQ process with no
pipelining the answer on a space link is always to pay for redundancy
before knowing whether it is needed. The assumption that produces that
conclusion is the no-pipelining one: a sender that runs enough
parallel HARQ processes to fill the round trip hides D entirely, and
then the crossover moves back to D near zero and retransmission wins
again. The crossover is a statement about one process, not about the
link.
