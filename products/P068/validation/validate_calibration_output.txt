On the censored units every model's curve runs far above the diagonal at
the low levels, which is the signature of predicting too short a
remaining life. The learned models saturate at 0.05 and 0.95 because
their CDF is an interpolation of seven fitted quantiles and cannot
resolve the tails; that is a property of the representation.
