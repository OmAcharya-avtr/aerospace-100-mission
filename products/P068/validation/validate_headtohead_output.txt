==============================================================================
3. the field-measurable part of censored non-coverage
==============================================================================
For a censored unit all that is known at analysis time is that the
remaining life exceeds the follow-up. An interval whose upper endpoint
is below that follow-up is refuted without waiting. The falsification
rate is a strict lower bound on censored non-coverage and is the only
part of it a deployment can measure.

model                     falsified  true non-cov     ratio
linear                       0.2203        0.6872    0.3205
exponential                  0.1762        0.6608    0.2667
powerlaw-fade                0.0088        0.9207    0.0096
learned-ipcw                 0.0396        0.6652    0.0596
learned-complete-case        0.0529        0.6828    0.0774

A deployment that only checked falsification would see a small fraction
of the censored failures. The rest is invisible until the units cross.

==============================================================================
quantity                              complete-case        IPCW      change
coverage, complete                           0.7514      0.7630     +0.0116

mean PIT, censored                           0.8043      0.7675     -0.0367
median true censored life [d]             1126.1599   1126.1599     +0.0000

IPCW re-weights the uncensored training units by the reciprocal of their
probability of having been followed long enough to be observed, so the
long-lived units carry more of the fit. It moves the censored numbers in
the right direction and costs accuracy on the complete ones. It does not
close the gap: the censored coverage remains far below nominal.

The censoring distribution here has a bounded support, so the estimated
G stays away from zero and the cap binds only at the smallest values.
On a fleet with heavier censoring the cap would matter more.
