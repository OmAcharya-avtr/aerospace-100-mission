  NOTE: the RMSE winner and the consistency winner are NOT the same method.
  Reporting only RMSE would have hidden that.

  IMPORTANT CAVEAT ON THE CONSISTENCY WINNER: the Mehra scheme is pinned at
  its upper clip on essentially every held-out run, and its correlation with
  the true scale is zero. It is therefore NOT adapting - it is applying a
  constant 64x inflation of Q. That makes the filter pessimistic (ANEES below
  the lower bound), which happens to land nearer dof = 2 than the optimistic
  alternatives. Calling it 'the consistency winner' without this sentence
  would be misleading, so the sentence is printed here and repeated in
  MODEL_CARD.md and README.md.
