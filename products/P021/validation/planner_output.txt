PART C -- the four required failure modes
==============================================================================
C1 infeasible geometry: a contiguous 15 deg band of cones around the
   y-z great circle, with the boresight starting at +x and asked for -x.
   Every continuous boresight path crosses the band, so no via point of
   any number can help. Both endpoints are clear by 75 deg, so this is
   genuinely a path-existence failure, not an endpoint failure.
    feasible=False  reason=no_feasible_path_found
    starts tried=0  path evaluations=0
    direct slew margin=-5.0000 deg

C2 wheel saturation mid-slew: sized with the scalar rule of thumb, the
   exact torque leaves the box part-way through the first segment.
    feasible=False  reason=wheel_torque_limit_exceeded
    torque utilisation   = 1.115744
    exact-envelope util  = 1.008464
    gyroscopic fraction  = 0.159090
    worst segment        = 0
    with envelope sizing : feasible=True, utilisation=1.000000, T=85.113 s
    the rule of thumb would have promised T=81.618 s, 4.11 % faster than is achievable

C3 cone tangency: a cone placed exactly tangent to the direct slew arc.
    offset -1.0e-06 deg: direct margin -0.000001000 deg, feasible=True, n_via=1
    offset +0.0e+00 deg: direct margin -0.000000000 deg, feasible=True, n_via=1
    offset +1.0e-06 deg: direct margin +0.000001000 deg, feasible=True, n_via=0
    The planner does not have to decide the tangent case correctly to
    be safe: a tangent arc has margin 0, which satisfies the >= 0
    constraint, and any required_margin > 0 pushes it off the boundary.

C4 the degenerate 180 deg slew: the eigenaxis is still unique, but the
   quaternion scalar part is zero and slerp is at its least conditioned.
     179.000 deg: slew angle 179.000000000 deg, feasible=True, T=53.721098 s, n_via=0
     179.999 deg: slew angle 179.999000000 deg, feasible=True, T=53.870798 s, n_via=0
     180.000 deg: slew angle 180.000000000 deg, feasible=True, T=53.870947 s, n_via=0
     180.001 deg: slew angle 179.999000000 deg, feasible=True, T=53.870798 s, n_via=0

   With a cone squarely across the 180 deg arc:

PART E -- settings sensitivity
==============================================================================
The SLSQP iteration cap and the cold-start deviation magnitudes are the
only tuning knobs in the planner. Measured over 14 seeded problems.

  maxiter        magnitudes    s/plan  feasible   mean T [s]  relative    evals
       40           (0.45,)     0.912  14 / 14         86.054    1.0000     1484
       25           (0.45,)     0.699  14 / 14         86.950    1.0110     1097
       15           (0.45,)     0.502  14 / 14         86.420    1.0042      719
       25            (0.6,)     0.732  14 / 14         87.016    1.0110     1124
       40        (0.3, 0.7)     1.500  14 / 14         85.521    0.9945     2869

The default is maxiter = 25 with a single 0.45 rad magnitude: it costs
about half the time of 40 iterations for a mean objective near 1 %
worse. Nothing here is random, so these figures reproduce exactly.
