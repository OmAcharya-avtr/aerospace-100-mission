
==============================================================================
PART A -- dataset and the cold baseline
==============================================================================
Problems are drawn Haar-uniformly on SO(3) with 1-3 Haar-uniform cones
of 15-50 deg half-angle, and kept only when both endpoints clear every
cone and the direct eigenaxis slew violates at least one. Labels are the
via parameter vector the cold multi-start planner arrives at: seven
deterministic SLSQP starts, best objective kept.

train :  199 labelled of 200 attempted (label rate 0.9950), 56.3 s wall, 2 worker processes
test  :  110 labelled of 110 attempted (label rate 1.0000), 29.3 s wall
features 28, targets 3

Cold baseline recorded during labelling (note: the labelling runs two
worker processes on two cores, so these solve times are inflated by the
contention. PART C re-times the cold planner single-process, and that is
the number the benchmark uses):
  mean solve time     : 509.52 ms (under 2-way parallelism)
  mean path evals     : 1024
  mean objective      : 86.4850 s

Target distribution (via parameter, canonical frame, rad):
  p1 (along eigenaxis)   mean -0.0628  sd 1.2205  min -3.1267  max +3.1186
  p2 (boresight plane)   mean -0.0061  sd 0.4446  min -1.6418  max +1.2733
  p3 (normal)            mean +0.0117  sd 0.3461  min -1.7454  max +1.2350
  |p| mean 1.2573 rad, max 3.1341 rad

PART B -- training and regression error

fit time            : 0.46 s
train  |p_pred - p_label| : mean 0.2590 rad, median 0.1644, p90 0.5782
test   |p_pred - p_label| : mean 0.8791 rad, median 0.5621, p90 1.8787
predicting zero      : mean 1.2528 rad (the trivial baseline the model must beat)
predicting the train mean: mean 1.2495 rad

Regression error is not the quantity of interest -- a warm start only
has to land in the right basin, not on the label -- but a model that
cannot beat 'predict zero' has learned nothing, so it is reported.

==============================================================================
PART C -- the benchmark
==============================================================================
Three planners on the same held-out problems:
  cold   the deterministic seven-start sweep, best objective kept
  warm   one SLSQP start from the model's prediction, no fallback
  zero   one SLSQP start from p = 0, no fallback -- the control that
         separates 'the model helps' from 'one start is enough'
  warm+  the model's start, falling back to the cold sweep on failure
         (this is what plan(warm_start=...) does by default)

planner     feasible   mean t [ms]   median t [ms]  mean evals   mean T [s]
cold       110 / 110          458.83          444.94        1024      86.4850
warm        86 / 110           68.46           66.00         150      89.8547
zero        89 / 110           68.34           63.56         148      89.2037
warm+      110 / 110          170.72           68.63         376      88.9235

model inference time per problem : 44.20 ms (included in the warm timings above)
warm start accepted without fallback : 86 / 110
warm-start paths violating a cone    : 0   tolerance 0

PART F -- compute budget
==============================================================================
train dataset generation (200 problems, 2 processes)       56.3 s   within the 180 s guide budget
test dataset generation  (110 problems, 2 processes)       29.3 s   within the 180 s guide budget
model fit                                                   0.5 s   within the 180 s guide budget
benchmark (110 problems x 4 planners)                      84.3 s   within the 180 s guide budget
total                                                     170.3 s

The label cost is what makes the dataset small: every training example
is a full cold solve. Peak resident memory is dominated by the 300-tree
forest, a few megabytes.

Process-pool efficiency: labelling took 281 ms
per problem on 2 workers against a single-process cold solve of 459 ms
(PART C). On a 2-core machine the pool buys little and sometimes nothing;
the figure is reported rather than assumed.
