
==============================================================================
PART A -- torque-free conservation of the inertial angular momentum
==============================================================================
Euler's equation with tau_ext = 0 conserves L = R(q) J omega exactly.
300 s at dt = 0.02 s, RK4, quaternion renormalised every step.

case                         |L| drift [N*m*s]      relative   dir drift [rad]
spin about max axis               1.332268e-15     3.331e-16      8.539566e-15
spin about int axis               8.881784e-16     1.776e-16      1.193045e-14
spin about min axis               3.552714e-15     5.921e-16      4.921288e-14
tumble (0.03,0.04,0.02)           2.664535e-14     4.746e-15      1.354099e-14
near-separatrix                   2.398082e-14     4.796e-15      3.124452e-14

worst relative |L| drift  : 4.796144e-15   tolerance 1e-12
worst direction drift     : 4.921288e-14 rad

The two columns are not the same quantity and do not have the same
error. |L| = |J omega| is an invariant of Euler's equation on its own,
independent of the attitude, and RK4 preserves it to round-off. The
*direction* of L in inertial space is L = R(q) J omega, so it inherits
the attitude truncation error, which is O(dt^4) and shown in PART B.
Reporting only the first number would be flattering and wrong.
