PART A -- hand-computed case
==============================================================================
Eigenaxis e = z. Boresight at psi = 0 is n0 = (sin 60, 0, cos 60),
so the boresight traces a small circle of angular radius 60 deg about z.
Cone axis c = x, half-angle gamma = 40 deg. Sweep 120 deg.

f(psi) = n(psi) . c = A cos psi + B sin psi + C with
  A = n0.c - (e.n0)(e.c),  B = (e x n0).c,  C = (e.n0)(e.c)
  e.n0  = cos 60 deg      = 0.5000000000000001
  e.c   = z . x           = 0.0
  n0.c  = sin 60 deg      = 0.8660254037844386
  A     = sqrt(3)/2       = 0.8660254037844386   (hand: 0.8660254037844386)
  B                       = 0.0
  C                       = 0.0
  |A - sqrt(3)/2|         = 0.000e+00   tolerance 1e-15

So f(psi) = (sqrt(3)/2) cos psi, maximised at psi = 0 with value
sqrt(3)/2 = cos 30 deg. The boresight is therefore 30 deg from the cone
axis at the start, and the cone is 40 deg, so the margin is exactly
-10 deg and the arc starts 10 deg inside the cone.
  library min margin      = -9.999999999999995 deg
  hand                    = -10 deg
  |difference|            = 5.329e-15 deg   tolerance 1e-13

Violation ends where f = cos gamma, i.e. cos psi = cos 40 / cos 30:
  cos 40 / cos 30         = 0.8845519308919177
  psi_exit = arccos(.)    = 0.4852638035810586 rad = 27.80356789566002 deg
  library intervals [deg] = [(0.0, 27.80356789566001)]
  |difference|            = 2.220e-16 rad   tolerance 1e-14
  reported depth          = 9.999999999999995 deg (hand: 10 deg)

PART B -- closed form vs dense sampling, 4000 random geometries
==============================================================================
The sampled reference evaluates the margin at 20 001 points per arc.
The closed form must never be larger than the sampled minimum (that
would be a missed violation) and must agree to the sampling resolution.

worst |closed form - sampled|   : 6.357903e-07 rad   tolerance 1e-6
worst (closed form - sampled)   : 1.332268e-15 rad (positive means the closed form was optimistic)
The sampled minimum can only be >= the true minimum, so a small positive
value here is the sampling error, not a defect in the closed form.

==============================================================================
PART F -- the sampling trap
==============================================================================
A planner that checks the path at N samples misses any violation
narrower than the sample spacing. Constructed case: a 90 deg sweep
whose boresight clips a cone for a stretch of the widths below.

The cone is centred at psi = 0.3712345 of the sweep, deliberately not on
a grid point of any of the sample counts below.

 violation width [deg]      N=51     N=201    N=1001   closed form
                 12.00    caught    caught    caught        caught
                  4.00    caught    caught    caught        caught
                  1.00    MISSED    caught    caught        caught
                  0.25    MISSED    caught    caught        caught
                  0.05    MISSED    MISSED    caught        caught

The closed form catches every one. This is the reason the planner's
constraint is analytic rather than sampled.
