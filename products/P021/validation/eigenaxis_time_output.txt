D2 -- bang-bang: what phase splitting is worth
The commanded torque jumps at T/2. A fixed-step RK4 that steps across
that instant weights the pre-switch acceleration into a post-switch step
and drops to first order. simulate_profile() therefore integrates each
phase separately (SlewProfile.switch_times). The naive single-interval
integration is run here for contrast; nothing in the library uses it.

==============================================================================
PART E -- the gyroscopic term the scalar sizing model drops
==============================================================================
tau_exact = psi_ddot J e + psi_dot^2 (e x J e);  the scalar model keeps
only the first term with |J e| replaced by e^T J e. The second term
vanishes if and only if e is a principal axis.

eigenaxis                       J_e     |J e|    |tau|max/J_e alpha   gyro fraction
z (principal)                80.000    80.000              1.000000        0.000000
x (principal)               120.000   120.000              1.000000        0.000000
(1,1,0)/sqrt2               110.000   110.454              1.014227        0.140797
(1,1,1)/sqrt3               100.000   101.325              1.045210        0.245415
(3,1,2)/|.|                 107.143   108.628              1.047272        0.250581

A slew about (1,1,0)/sqrt2 of this spacecraft therefore needs more
torque than the rule of thumb budgets, at the middle of the slew,
exactly where the rate is highest.
