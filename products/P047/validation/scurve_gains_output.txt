   notable rows, all measured not assumed:
   - early-late on rect: K_d = 0. A rectangle is flat inside the pulse, so the
     difference of two gates inside it is identically zero and no loop exists.
   - mueller-muller on rect and on half-sine: K_d = 0. The detector is driven by
     h(+-1); a rectangle is flat there and a half-sine is zero there.
   - mueller-muller on rc-time: cd/fit far from 1. h'(+-1) = 0 for that shape, so
     the S-curve is third order at the origin and the gain is ill-conditioned.
   - gardner on rect: cd/fit far from 1 for the same reason in reverse - the pulse
     is discontinuous, so the S-curve is a step at the origin.
