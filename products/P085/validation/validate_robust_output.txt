==============================================================================
3. outlier identification against the injected mask
==============================================================================
Scored on the ROWS, against ground truth from pointmodel.synthetic. Huber
downweights continuously, so a row counts as downweighted when its final
weight is below 0.5; RANSAC's weights are 0 or 1 by construction.

Huber downweights the Gaussian tail too, by design: at delta = 1.345 a
standard normal has 2(1 - Phi(1.345)) = 0.1786 of its mass beyond the
threshold, so a clean false-positive rate near 0.18 at weight < 1 is
expected and is not an error. The 0.5 cut is the one reported.

A row-wise recall below one is EXPECTED and is not a miss. The failure
displacement is 300 arcsec in a uniformly random direction, so its
cross-elevation component is 300 cos(angle) and its elevation component is
300 sin(angle); when the angle is near a right angle one of the two
components is arbitrarily small and that row is indistinguishable from a
clean one by any method. The meaningful figure is the OBSERVATION-wise
recall, counting an acquisition as found when either of its rows is
flagged, and that is reported beside it.
