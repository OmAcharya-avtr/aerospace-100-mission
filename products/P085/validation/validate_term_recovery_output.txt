1. single-term recovery, noiseless, full_sky geometry
==============================================================================
The truth is generated by the exact rotation composition of equation (12)
and fitted with the first-order design matrix, so the error left is the
truncation and nothing else. Its ORDER differs from term to term, and the
order is derivable rather than measured:

  IA, IE, TF, TS, RF -- EXACT, to rounding. IA alone is a rotation about the
    untilted vertical by exactly IA, so the beam is the nominal beam at
    azimuth A + IA and dX = IA cos(E) with no remainder. IE, TF, TS and RF
    enter only through the effective elevation of equation (13), so the beam
    is the nominal beam at elevation E + dE_el, giving dE = dE_el and dX = 0
    with no remainder. These five have NO truncation at all and their error
    must stay at machine precision and must NOT grow with the amplitude.
  AN, AW -- QUADRATIC. A finite rotation of a unit vector departs from its
    first-order approximation at second order, and no other column absorbs
    the departure, so the coefficient error scales as the square.
  CA, NPAE -- CUBIC. Both appear only in the cross-elevation block, and
    there the exact expressions are
        dX(CA)   = cos(E) arctan(tan(CA) / cos(E)) = CA - (CA^3/3) tan^2(E) + ...
        dX(NPAE) = cos(E) arctan(sin(NPAE) tan(E)) = NPAE sin(E) + O(NPAE^3)
    so the second-order part of their effect falls in the elevation block,
    where the elevation-side columns absorb it, and what is left in the
    coefficient is third order.

The amplitude ratios below are 5 and 4, so quadratic gives 25 and 16 and
cubic gives 125 and 64.

two_az     9                    9.7139e-02                      1.3793e-02                             2.0152e-03                  

The rank column is the numerical rank of the whitened design matrix at the
solver's rcond of 1e-12, not the identifiability verdict, whose declared
threshold is 1e-3 on the singular value ratio. narrow_el has full numerical
rank and is still refused: its smallest ratio is 1.5e-6, which is above the
solver's cut and three orders of magnitude below the identifiability one.

The coefficient error is large where the geometry is restricted and the predicted
sky error is small everywhere: a rank-deficient geometry reproduces the
residual field it was given and attributes it wrongly. That is the whole
problem this product addresses, and it is visible in the two columns above.

[PASS] a rank-deficient geometry has a large coefficient error -- which is why the per-term split is refused there
