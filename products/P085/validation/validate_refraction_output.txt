With the RADIO DRY constant the largest disagreement is 8.4736 arcsec, which

which at 10 degrees elevation is 5.7 arcsec and is not negligible for a
pointing model.

==============================================================================
3. the plane-parallel limit of the refraction integral
==============================================================================
The flat-atmosphere limit of equation (4) is (n0 - 1) tan(z), which needs no
quadrature. The departure of the spherical integral from it is derivable
without running either: Snell's invariant gives
  sin(zeta) = sin(z) / (1 + h/R) * (n0/n),
so to leading order d(ln sin zeta) = -h/R, hence d(zeta) = -(h/R) tan(zeta)
and d(tan zeta)/tan(zeta) = -(h/R) sec^2(zeta). Weighted at the refractivity
scale height H = R_specific T0 / g, the predicted relative departure is
-(H/R_earth) sec^2(z).
