==============================================================================
the model is exact for an exponential block
==============================================================================

  For a constant hazard rate, sum(duration * duty * stress) IS the
  integral of the hazard rate over the mission, so the effective-age
  model is exact rather than approximate. The reference is the integral,
  evaluated by quad over the piecewise-constant stress profile.

==============================================================================
a one-shot block is not aged by a profile
==============================================================================

  A FixedProbability block's figure of merit is a probability per
  actuation, not a rate. Multiplying it by a duty cycle is not a model
  of anything, so a profile that tries is refused.
