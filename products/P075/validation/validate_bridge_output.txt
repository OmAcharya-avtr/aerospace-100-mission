sourcing, stated before any number
==============================================================================

  DERIVED IN THIS REPOSITORY:
    R = 2p^5 - 5p^4 + 2p^3 + 2p^2, by pivotal decomposition on the
    bridge edge e3:

      with e3 working, vertices a and b merge, giving two parallel
      pairs in series: (1 - (1-p)^2)^2 = 4p^2 - 4p^3 + p^4
      with e3 failed, two disjoint paths in parallel:
      1 - (1 - p^2)^2 = 2p^2 - p^4

      R = p(4p^2 - 4p^3 + p^4) + (1-p)(2p^2 - p^4)
        = 4p^3 - 4p^4 + p^5 + 2p^2 - p^4 - 2p^3 + p^5
        = 2p^5 - 5p^4 + 2p^3 + 2p^2

    At p = 0.9 this is 2(0.59049) - 5(0.6561) + 2(0.729) + 2(0.81)
                     = 1.18098 - 3.2805 + 1.458 + 1.62 = 0.97848

  CITED FOR THE METHOD ONLY:
    Barlow, R. E. and Proschan, F. (1975), Statistical Theory of
    Reliability and Life Testing, Holt, Rinehart and Winston, chapter 1,
    for the pivotal decomposition of a coherent structure.
    Billinton, R. and Allan, R. N., Reliability Evaluation of
    Engineering Systems, 2nd edition, Plenum Press, 1992, for the bridge
    network and the conditional-probability method applied to it.

  NOT CITED:
    No page number and no published figure. This build had no access to
    either volume. A page number that cannot be checked is worse than
    none, so none is given, and the derivation above stands in its place.

==============================================================================
the bridge is not series-parallel
==============================================================================

  This is why the bridge is the right check. No nesting of series and
  parallel groups reproduces it, and the two decompositions a reader
  might reach for BRACKET the true answer rather than giving it.

         p    two disjoint paths          exact bridge        merged a and b
  --------------------------------------------------------------------------
     0.100     0.019900000000000     0.021520000000000     0.036100000000000
     0.500     0.437500000000000     0.500000000000000     0.562500000000000
     0.900     0.963900000000000     0.978480000000000     0.980100000000000
     0.990     0.999603990000000     0.999798049800000     0.999800010000000

  Treating e3 as always failed gives the lower figure; treating it as
  always working gives the upper one. The exact answer is strictly
  between them at every p in (0, 1), which is the definition of a
  structure that is not series-parallel.
  [PASS] the exact bridge reliability lies strictly between the two series-parallel figures

==============================================================================
self-duality
==============================================================================

  The bridge's minimal cut sets and minimal path sets have the same
  shape -- two of size 2 and two of size 3 -- so the structure is
  self-dual and R(p) + R(1-p) = 1 exactly. This is a property of the
  polynomial that no part of the implementation was written to satisfy,
  so it is a check on the polynomial itself.
