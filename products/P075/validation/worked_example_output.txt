importance measures, on a diagram that admits them
==============================================================================

  The smallsat bus contains a standby node, whose quantification is not
  the independent-event arithmetic over its structure function, so
  importance measures are refused for it. The voting triple is Boolean
  throughout and admits them.

  smallsat bus: importance measures are not defined for this diagram: it contains ['standby'] node(s). Those have an exact Boolean structure function but a dependent quantification, so dQ_sys/dq_i is not the derivative of the number quantify() returns. Returning a figure whose meaning differs from its name would be worse than refusing.
