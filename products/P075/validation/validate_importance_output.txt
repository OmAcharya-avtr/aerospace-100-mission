Birnbaum against central finite-difference perturbation
==============================================================================

  I_B(i) = dQ_sys / dq_i, scored against
  [Q_sys(q_i + h) - Q_sys(q_i - h)] / (2h).

  Q_sys is multilinear in the q_i for a coherent structure of
  independent components, so the derivative is constant in q_i and the
  finite difference is exact for ANY step in exact arithmetic. The only
  error is the cancellation in the numerator, which grows as the step
  shrinks -- which is why the agreement is measured across five decades
  of step size rather than at one.

  declared tolerance : 1.0e-06 relative

==============================================================================
exact Fussell-Vesely against the rare-event approximation
==============================================================================

  The rare-event approximation replaces P(union of cut sets containing i)
  by the SUM of their probabilities, which overestimates. It is what most
  tools report, and it is the usual reason a published Fussell-Vesely
  figure exceeds 1. This package computes the union exactly, by
  enumerating the states of the events involved. The gap is on record
  here rather than left implicit.
