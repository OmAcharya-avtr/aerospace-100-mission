greedy's retained fraction above 1.0 is not an error. Greedy's nominal selection
is not optimal, so a perturbation that reshuffles its density ordering can land
on a better selection than the unperturbed ordering did, and the fraction is
measured against greedy's own unperturbed value. The exact solver has no such
headroom: its unperturbed selection is the optimum, so its retained fraction
cannot exceed 1.0 and the amount by which it falls below is the cost of noise.
