==============================================================================
2. the uncertainty output
==============================================================================
the forest reports the standard deviation of its per-tree predictions. This is a
dispersion measure over the ensemble and NOT a calibrated confidence interval:
the trees share training data, so the spread understates predictive uncertainty
(Wager, Hastie and Efron 2014). The measured coverage below is what that costs.

        band  nominal normal  measured coverage
   +-1 sigma          0.6827           0.688889
+-1.96 sigma          0.9500           0.941111
   +-2 sigma          0.9545           0.947222

mean dispersion                       : 1.175507 value units
mean absolute error                   : 0.962341 value units
Spearman(dispersion, |error|)          : 0.601302
a positive rank correlation means the dispersion is informative about where the
model is wrong even though its band is not calibrated, which is the useful
property: a product the ensemble disagrees about is a product to check by hand
