==============================================================================
2. the entropy floor is not a bound for a correlated source
==============================================================================
Shannon's source-coding theorem bounds the ratio below by H/B for a MEMORYLESS
source. The generator's equation (7) includes a correlation gain, as a real
codec does, so the realised ratio is allowed to fall below the floor. The
fraction below is therefore the fraction for which the analytic bound does not
apply, and is a statement about the generator and about real codecs alike.
