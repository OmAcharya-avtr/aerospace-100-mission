0  the bounds, and why each slack is what it is
==============================================================================
  Markov slack      : 4*eps*bound. The bound is a quotient of two positive quantities, so its relative condition number is 1 in each argument and the only amplification is the division. The slack vanishes with the bound.
  Cantelli slack    : 8*eps*((|D|+|mean|)/|D-mean|)*bound. The ill-conditioned step is the subtraction D - mean; the slack is its relative condition number times the bound's own magnitude. No floor.
  Bonferroni slack  : 4*eps*max(upper, observed, lower). Every quantity is a sum of 0/1 over a common denominator, so each carries one rounding of the division. The slack is zero on an all-zero indicator.
  double-precision eps: 2.220446e-16
