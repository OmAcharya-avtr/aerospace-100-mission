MEASUREMENT MODEL. Period T, implicit deadline D = T. Job i is released at t0 + i*T and its response time is end - release. A job whose response exceeds D is a MISS. If a job finishes after later releases have already passed, those releases never execute: they are DROPPED, and each dropped release is also a miss. Every executed row carries dropped_before, so releases = sum(1 + dropped_before) and misses = sum(missed + dropped_before) are both reconstructible from the committed traces.
