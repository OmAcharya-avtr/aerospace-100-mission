EVERYTHING IS SCORED ON THE SHORTFALL OF A PASS IN Mbit, which is this
product's headline quantity. The primary score is the CRPS, which is
strictly proper over a whole predictive distribution; a point forecast is
the one-member ensemble, for which the CRPS is the absolute error exactly
(Gneiting & Raftery 2007, JASA 102(477), section 4.2).

CALIBRATION IS REPORTED BESIDE THE ERROR and is the figure that decides
whether a forecast is usable, because the product's value is a
distribution a scheduler books against. PIT cal err is the Kolmogorov
distance of the empirical probability integral transform from the uniform
(Dawid 1984; Gneiting, Balabdaoui & Raftery 2007). cov80 is the coverage
of the nominal 80 per cent interval: the target is 0.800 and EITHER
direction away from it is a defect.
