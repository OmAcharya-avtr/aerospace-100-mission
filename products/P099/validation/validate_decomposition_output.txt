the identity closes to the QUADRATURE, not to the rounding: each term is a
composite Simpson sum over its OWN partition of the window, and four sums
over four partitions do not telescope. Computing the losses and then
DEFINING the realised yield as the capacity minus them would force rho = 0
by construction and the identity would be untestable.
