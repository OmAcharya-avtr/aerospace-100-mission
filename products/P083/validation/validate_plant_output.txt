3. the zero-order-hold matrices of equation (7), three ways
==============================================================================
Van Loan's block exponential, against the inverse formula A^-1 (A_d - I) B
and against composite Simpson quadrature of the defining integral. The
second and third use mathematics the implementation does not: the inverse
formula needs A invertible, which the implementation deliberately avoids,
and the quadrature knows nothing about matrix exponentials beyond expm.

7. the integrator floor: the divergence that is not a divergence
==============================================================================

exactly zero. The with-miss branch nevertheless integrates the period in two
pieces and the reference integrates it in one, so their fixed-step truncation
errors differ. That difference is the FLOOR below which no divergence
measurement means anything, and it is measured here rather than assumed.
