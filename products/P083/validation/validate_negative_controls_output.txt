5. the learned pipeline, against shuffled labels and blinded features
==============================================================================
A model whose labels have been shuffled must lose its skill, and a model
blinded to the two features the certificate uses must get measurably worse.
Both are scored at the SAME flag rate so the comparison is of recall and
not of operating point.

The statistic is the SMALLEST flag rate at which a model still catches
every consequential miss on the held-out runs. A model that has lost
information has to flag more to catch the same set. Comparing at a fixed
flag rate would not work: at a flag rate of 0.25 all three models reach a
recall of 1 on this population and the comparison is saturated, which is
how an earlier version of this control came to be too weak to fire.
