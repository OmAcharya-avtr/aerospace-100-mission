state-dependent input and uses the sub-additivity of the logarithmic norm.

==============================================================================

can be checked rather than believed. Van Loan's method involves no norm

6. where the conservatism comes from: long holds
==============================================================================
Equation (9) grows like exp(mu_S tau) while the measured divergence of this
plant grows roughly linearly in tau, because mu_S is large here as an
artifact of the anisotropic metric and not because the dynamics expand. The
consequence is a bound that is within a small factor of the truth for a
sub-period overrun and
enormously conservative for a hold of several periods. That is measured
here, against re-simulation, at the three largest input steps in a declared
run.
