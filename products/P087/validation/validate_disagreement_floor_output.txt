highest crossing, the floor : 11.24480 deg
ratio                      : 9.179

A bisection that assumed a single crossing and walked up from the horizon
would stop at the lowest one and understate the floor by that ratio. The
non-monotonicity is caused by members LEAVING the ensemble: the undefined
count column shows plane_parallel and oriani_series dropping out at their
own validity limits, and the spread falls when the most discrepant member
goes. The floor reported by this package is always the HIGHEST crossing.
[PASS] the disagreement curve is measured to be non-monotone and the floor is the highest crossing -- 3 crossings, floor 11.24480 deg against lowest crossing 1.22500 deg
