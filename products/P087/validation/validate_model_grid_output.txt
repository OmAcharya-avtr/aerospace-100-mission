The sign column is the finding. Above 15 degrees the plane-parallel model and
both published fits OVERSTATE the refraction relative to the layered integral
at every one of the 54 swept states, and the Oriani series understates it by
a few hundredths of an arcsecond. Below about 7 degrees the sign of the
published fits' error stops being constant across the state grid: the same
formula is high in one atmosphere and low in another, so the departure cannot
be corrected by a scale factor.
