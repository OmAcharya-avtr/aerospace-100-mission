The second block is the honest limit of the whole method: a profile
interval is computed under the assumption that the calibration model is
the model that generated the data. When it is not, the interval is a
statement about the best fit of the wrong model and its coverage of the
true parameters collapses. No amount of profiling repairs that; only a
better model does.
