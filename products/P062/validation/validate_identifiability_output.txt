==============================================================================

  design                smallest s/s_max   decades from 1e-8
  baseline-eclipse            2.5190e-03                5.40
  full-sun                    1.0562e-11               -2.98
  lumped-nodes                2.8160e-03                5.45
  coarse-sampled              2.5399e-03                5.40
  weak-excitation             4.2156e-06                2.62

Read this table honestly. Within two decades of the default cut -- that
is, for tol in [1e-10, 1e-6] -- every verdict is stable: full-sun is rank
5 and the other four are rank 6. Outside that band two verdicts do move,
and both moves are expected from the spectrum above:
  tol = 1e-12 puts the cut BELOW full-sun's structural null at 1.06e-11,
    so that design is then called full rank. The cut has gone past the
    accuracy of the differentiation itself (validate_sensitivity.py check
    2 measures that floor at about 5e-08 relative), so 1e-12 is not a
    tolerance this machinery can support.
  tol = 1e-05 puts the cut ABOVE weak-excitation's smallest ratio of
    4.22e-06, so that design is then called rank deficient. That is a
    defensible reading of a design whose relative standard deviations run
    to several hundred per cent; it is a change of reporting threshold,
    not a disagreement about the numbers.
The default 1e-08 is two decades above the measured differentiation floor
and three decades above the structural null it has to detect.
