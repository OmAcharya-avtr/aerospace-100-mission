6. Per-parameter sensitivity measure on every design
==============================================================================
delta_msqr = ||J_w[:, j]|| / sqrt(n_obs): the root-mean-square change in
the data, in units of the measurement noise, for one natural log unit of
the parameter (Brun et al. 2001).

Every parameter moves the data by many noise standard deviations per log
unit on every design, which is why the MAGNITUDE of the sensitivity is
not what goes wrong here. What goes wrong is collinearity, and that is
what validate_identifiability.py measures.
