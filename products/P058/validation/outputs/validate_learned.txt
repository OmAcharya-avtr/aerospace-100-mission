0. Problem, baseline and split
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Task        : given a trailing window of 50 standardised telemetry
                samples, score the proposition 'a change occurred in or
                just before this window'.
  Baseline    : the five analytic detectors, implemented and calibrated
                FIRST. The comparison is at equal measured ARL0 and is in
                validate_change_types.py section 6.
  Split       : by SEED, not by row. Training seeds [58001, 58002, 58003, 58004, 58005, 58006, 58007, 58008];
                test seeds [58051, 58052, 58053, 58054, 58055, 58056]. A row split would leak:
                consecutive windows overlap by 49 of 50 samples, so
                neighbouring rows are nearly identical and a random row
                split would put near-copies on both sides. This is the
                single most important methodological choice here.
  Labels      : 1 when the window's right edge is in [change, change+50).
  Positives   : from 7 declared change specs: (('mean_step', 0.5), ('mean_step', 1.0), ('mean_step', 2.0), ('variance_step', 1.8), ('variance_step', 3.0), ('drift_ramp', 0.01), ('drift_ramp', 0.03))
  Negatives   : stationary streams only. Transients are HELD OUT, so the
                transient experiment is fair to all six detectors.

  Why, and what follows from it:
  * The split is by seed, so a held-out stream shares no samples with any
    training stream. A depth-8 forest with 100 trees over 78000 windows
    has enough capacity to fit the particular noise realisations of eight
    seeds, and ROC-AUC on the training rows reflects that.
  * The honest number is therefore the HELD-OUT one, and it is the only
    one quoted anywhere outside this paragraph. A reader who sees ROC-AUC
    0.941 quoted for this model is being shown the training
    figure.
  * No retuning was performed to close the gap. Reducing depth or trees
    until the gap closed would produce a prettier number for the same
    stream-level result, which is already a loss at equal ARL0, and this
    portfolio's policy is that an honest negative is not retuned away.

  FINDING  THE LEARNED MODEL OVERFITS AT THE WINDOW LEVEL: train ROC-AUC 0.941 against held-out 0.823, a gap of +0.118 against a pre-declared expectation of under 0.05; average precision 0.634 against 0.391. The split is by seed so this is not leakage, it is capacity. Not retuned: the stream-level result is already a loss at equal ARL0 and closing the gap would only change the window-level number.

  worst absolute reliability gap over the populated bins: 0.5215

  The score is NOT claimed to be calibrated and this is the evidence. A
  forest vote fraction on a class-weighted, heavily imbalanced problem is
  an ordering, not a probability. It is used in this package only as a
  monotone score whose threshold is moved until the measured ARL0 matches
  a target, which needs no calibration at all. A reader who wants a
  calibrated probability should recalibrate it; a sibling product in this
  portfolio (P056 CalibAudit) is the one that does that, and it is not
  imported here.
  FINDING  the learned detector's confidence output is MISCALIBRATED: the worst gap between mean predicted score and observed frequency over the populated bins is 0.522. The package uses the score only as a monotone ordering whose threshold is set by measured ARL0, which does not require calibration, and the README does not claim a probability.

7. What this model is not
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  It is not certified for operational flight use.
  It is trained entirely on synthetic i.i.d. Gaussian streams with
  injected changes of four declared kinds. It has never seen real
  spacecraft telemetry, and validate_robustness.py section 1 shows what
  autocorrelation alone does to the operating point of every detector
  here, learned or analytic.
  It loses to EWMA and CUSUM at equal ARL0 on three of the four change
  types it was trained on, and ties on the fourth.
  Its confidence output is not calibrated (section 3).
  Its online cost is four orders of magnitude above the analytic
  recursions with this API on this container (validate_cost.py s.3).
