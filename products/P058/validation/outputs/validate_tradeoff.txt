7. Non-monotonicity: where a wider threshold detects FASTER
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  For a memoryless detector ARL1 must increase with ARL0: a less
  sensitive detector cannot be quicker. A windowed detector breaks this,
  because at a tight threshold it false-alarms often, resets, and spends
  its time in warm-up where it cannot detect anything at all.
