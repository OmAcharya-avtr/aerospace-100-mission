# Dataset card — telemdrift synthetic telemetry streams 0.1.0

Every stream used anywhere in this repository is **synthetic**, generated by
committed seeded scripts. **No real spacecraft telemetry was used, at any point,
for any purpose.** Nothing in this repository has been tested against a real
channel.

## What the data is

A univariate, standardised telemetry channel. The baseline process is
independent and identically distributed Gaussian noise:

```
x_t ~ N(mu_t, sigma_t^2),   x_t independent of x_s for t != s
```

The channel is dimensionless. A real channel — a transponder output power in
dBm, a battery bus voltage in V, a reaction-wheel current in A — is standardised
by the caller against its own declared nominal mean and nominal standard
deviation before entering the package. Change magnitudes are therefore in units
of the **pre-change standard deviation**, which is the only scale on which
detector thresholds transfer between channels.

Generator: `telemdrift.streams`, from `numpy.random.Generator` instances created
with explicit integer seeds.

## Change types

| Kind | Parameter | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `mean_step` | magnitude | step added to the mean, in pre-change σ |
| `variance_step` | magnitude | multiplicative factor on σ (2.0 doubles it) |
| `drift_ramp` | magnitude | slope in σ per sample |
| `transient` | magnitude, duration | peak amplitude and length of an excursion the channel **recovers from** |

`transient` is a **negative control**. There is no change in it, so every alarm
raised on it is a false alarm. It is in the same list as the three real changes
so that it is scored in the same tables.

An AR(1) variant, `ar1_stationary(length, seed, phi)`, generates a stream with
the **same marginal distribution** `N(0,1)` and a declared lag-one
autocorrelation. It exists so that the effect of dependence alone can be
isolated, and `validation/validate_robustness.py` measures it.

## How much data, and where it goes

Nothing is committed as a data file. Every stream is regenerated from its seed,
deterministically, when a script runs. The committed artifacts are the scripts,
their raw output and the figures.

| Use | Seeds | Volume per run |
|---|---|---|
| Threshold calibration | 58101–58106 | 6 × 30 000 = 180 000 samples per ARL0 evaluation, × ~20 evaluations per detector |
| Held-out ARL0 | 58201–58208 | 8 × 50 000 = 400 000 samples per detector |
| Trade-off sweep | 58301–58304 | 4 × 25 000 = 100 000 samples per point, 36 points |
| ARL1 replicates | 58400 + i | 300 streams × 2 500 samples per (detector, change) |
| Learned training | 58001–58008 | 78 104 windows of 50 samples |
| Learned test | 58051–58056 | 58 578 windows, seeds disjoint from training |
| Known-answer ARL0 | 59001–59012 | 12 × 60 000 = 720 000 samples |

Seed sets are disjoint where disjointness matters, and the disjointness is
asserted rather than assumed: `calibrate_threshold` raises if the calibration
and evaluation seed sets intersect, and `validate_environment.py` checks the
shipped configuration.

## Labels, for the learned component

A window is labelled 1 when its right edge lies in
`[change_index, change_index + horizon)` with `horizon = 50`, and 0 otherwise.

**The horizon defines the task.** The model is being asked "did a change occur
in the last 50 samples", and no label convention can let it detect faster than
the convention allows. `horizon = window` is used so a positive window always
contains at least one post-change sample.

Positive windows come from seven change specifications spanning the severities
the benchmark scores: mean steps of 0.5, 1.0 and 2.0 σ; variance steps of ×1.8
and ×3.0; drift ramps of 0.01 and 0.03 σ per sample. Negatives come from
stationary streams only.

Measured class balance: **3.58 % positive** in both the training and the
held-out set.

## Limitations of the data, which are limitations of every result here

1. **Independence is an assumption, not a property of telemetry.** Housekeeping
   channels are sampled faster than the physical time constants they measure and
   are strongly autocorrelated. `validation/VALIDATION.md` section 8.1 measures
   what that does: at lag-one autocorrelation 0.9, with the marginal
   distribution unchanged, the measured ARL0 at the calibrated thresholds falls
   by 28.8x (CUSUM), 34.5x (EWMA), 5.9x (ADWIN), 5.5x (Page-Hinkley) and 1.5x
   (windowed KS).
2. **Gaussianity is an assumption.** Real channels have quantisation floors,
   saturation, telemetry gaps, duplicated frames and bit errors. None is
   modelled.
3. **Univariate by construction.** Real faults appear across several channels at
   once, and the cross-channel structure is exactly what a multivariate method
   would exploit. Out of scope here.
4. **The change is instantaneous and isolated.** One change per stream, at a
   known index, with no second change and no recovery (except the transient,
   where recovery is the point).
5. **Training and test data come from the same generator.** The held-out split
   is by seed, which controls for memorised noise realisations but not for a
   mis-specified process. A model that matches this generator's structure is not
   thereby shown to match a spacecraft's.
6. **The quantisation limitation is real and measured in a different form.** The
   KS statistic is rank-based and exactly invariant under a common increasing
   map in real arithmetic, but not in floating point: `0.5 * 2.22e-16 + 1.0`
   rounds to `1.0`, turning two distinct samples into a tie. A channel quantised
   near its noise floor produces many ties and the KS detector's behaviour there
   has not been characterised.

## Regeneration

```bash
python -c "
from telemdrift.streams import ChangeSpec, change_stream, stationary, ar1_stationary
print(stationary(5, 58501))
print(change_stream(3, 3, ChangeSpec('mean_step', 1.0), 58501))
print(ar1_stationary(5, 58501, phi=0.8))
"
python examples/change_types.py   # the figure, with per-panel statistics printed
```

Determinism is checked, not assumed:
`validation/outputs/validate_learned.txt` section 5 records that the training
feature matrix regenerates bit-for-bit from its seeds.
