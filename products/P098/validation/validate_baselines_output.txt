THE EXACTNESS IS NOT A TAUTOLOGY, AND IT IS NOT INDEPENDENT EITHER. Said
plainly: the closure comparison and the ground truth implement the SAME
definition of staleness. They differ in representation -- one compares
SHA-256 digests of contents reconstructed from the event log at two
revisions, the other compares the content values themselves, and the
committed labels were written by the generator before this package read
them. So the agreement tests the closure search, the replay and the
hashing against each other and against the generator; it does not test
the definition, and no measurement could. The independent check on the
definition is the bracket in validate_bound_bracket.py, whose bound comes
from counting arguments this implementation does not use.

5. THE COMMITTED LABELS, AND THE AGGREGATE-MATCHES-ITEMS TRAP
==============================================================================

[PASS] the committed by-construction labels agree with the replay item by item

An aggregate stale fraction can be right while every item is wrong. On the
hand example in tests/test_baselines.py the timestamp baseline reports 0.5
against a true 0.5 with an agreement of 0.0. On this corpus:
  baseline1_timestamp          fraction 0.462500  error +0.058333  agreement 0.462500
  baseline1b_timestamp_depth1  fraction 0.820833  error +0.416667  agreement 0.475000

so the aggregate error of the timestamp baseline understates how wrong it
is per item, and the two error directions partly cancel in the aggregate.
That cancellation is the reason this product reports both directions.
