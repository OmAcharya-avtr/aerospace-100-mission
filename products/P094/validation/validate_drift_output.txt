MEASUREMENT MODEL
-----------------
  Hardware-mode figures come from the committed campaign in data/traces/, run on
  a Jetson Orin Nano: nine grid cells, 60 passes of 1500 frames each, 90000 timed
  frames per cell, 810000 frames in all, with the cell order randomised under seed
  20261115. Each of the six stages is bracketed individually by
  time.perf_counter_ns, whose resolution the campaign measured at 1.000 ns and
  whose empty-bracket cost it measured per cell at 107.365 to 115.204 ns. A pass
  summary is computed over EVERY timed frame of that pass; data/traces/stages.csv
  is subsampled every third frame and any figure taken from it says so. EVERY
  HARDWARE FIGURE HERE IS A FIGURE AT AN UNPINNED, SCHEDUTIL-GOVERNED CLOCK IN
  MAXN_SUPER: passwordless sudo was unavailable on the board, so neither the
  governor nor the power mode could be pinned, per-core frequency spanned 729600
  to 1728000 kHz across the campaign, and the share of per-stage variance that
  co-varies with core frequency is measured and reported rather than assumed away.
  The board also drifted: a fixed 160x160 float64 matmul reference, sharing no code
  with the pipeline, measured 141844.5 ns before the first cell and 152517.0 ns
  after the last, a ratio of 1.0752408447278534, and every divergence ratio is
  reported raw AND drift-adjusted with the raw figure as the headline.
  Simulation-mode figures are measured in the analysis container, now, by the same
  instrumented driver over the same committed pipeline file, and move between runs
  because they are wall-clock readings.

==============================================================================
a DEFECT in this product's own Spearman, found and corrected
==============================================================================
  Found by the coordinator's independent check on 2026-11-16, not by this build.

    published before the fix : 0.5333333333333333   WRONG
    corrected value         : 0.5809475019311126

  MECHANISM. The original implementation ranked with numpy.argsort(numpy.argsort(x)),
  which is an ORDINAL rank: it breaks ties by position in the array. W takes three
  values over the nine cells -- two 48s, five 64s and two 96s -- so the five 64s
  received five distinct ranks instead of five copies of their midrank. Spearman's
  rho is defined on midranks, so the published figure was a Pearson correlation on
  an arbitrary ordering and not Spearman's rho.

  It was not even well defined. The ordinal form is not invariant under a permutation
  of the rows: on this campaign it returns 0.85 with the cells in EXECUTION order
  and 0.5333333333333333 with them in the alphabetical order this
  function's groupby happens to produce, from identical data. The midrank form
  returns 0.5809475019311126 either way.

  The Pearson figure was unaffected and is unchanged, which is what made the defect
  diagnosable: the disagreement was isolated to the rank treatment.

[PASS] the superseded ordinal form is reproduced exactly, in both row orders -- alphabetical 0.5333333333333333, execution 0.8499999999999999
[PASS] the corrected midrank form IS invariant under that same reordering -- a rank statistic must not depend on the order of the observations
[PASS] the corrected Spearman matches the pinned value -- pinned 0.5809475019311126, measured 0.5809475019311126
[PASS] the corrected Spearman is FURTHER from the < 0.2 the SPEC §8 claim needs -- the finding is unchanged in direction and STRENGTHENED in magnitude by the correction; nothing was retuned to recover the old number
[FINDING] A DEFECT IN THIS PRODUCT, found by the coordinator's independent check and corrected. The published Spearman rho was 0.5333333333333333, computed with ordinal ranks from argsort(argsort(x)) on a series with five tied values. The correct tie-averaged-midrank value is 0.5809475019311126, which agrees with scipy.stats.spearmanr to 1e-12. The Pearson figure was unaffected. THE CONCLUSION IS UNCHANGED IN DIRECTION AND STRENGTHENED IN MAGNITUDE: the corrected rho is further from the < 0.2 that SPEC §8's decorrelation claim needs, not closer to it.

[FINDING] A CHECK THAT FAILS, published as failing. SPEC §8 randomised the cell order so that elapsed time would not be confounded with frame dimension, and the brief states that drift is therefore NOT confounded with frame dimension. Measured, corr(order_index, W) is 0.5012 Pearson and 0.5809 Spearman on midranks. That is not near zero. With nine cells and one draw under seed 20261115, the randomisation removed P093's SYSTEMATIC ascending-dimension ordering but left a substantial positive association in this particular draw.
