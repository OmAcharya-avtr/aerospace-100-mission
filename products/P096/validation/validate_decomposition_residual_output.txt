ACCOUNTING MODEL. A handover is a four-phase timeline from the break of the outgoing optical link: ACQUISITION [0, T_acq) with zero egress; RESTART [T_acq, T_o) with zero egress, T_o = T_acq + T_rst; then DRAIN [T_o, H), in which the egress ramps linearly 0 -> R_d over T_ramp and holds at R_d. A source of mean rate R_s feeds a tail-drop buffer of capacity B holding q0 at the break. Every dropped bit is attributed to the phase of the slot in which it was dropped, so the three mechanisms partition the window exactly. The TOTAL is computed a second way, from mass conservation alone (produced - delivered - (q_end - q0)), and the difference between the two is the DECOMPOSITION RESIDUAL, which is a reported number and not an assumption.
