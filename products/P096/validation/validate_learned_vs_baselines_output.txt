  BASELINE 2, THE ANALYTIC FLUID MODEL, WINS ON BOTH SPLITS. That is the
  finding and it is published as such. It is not retuned away and the learned
  component is not removed: a reader needs both numbers to know what the
  learned component bought, which here is nothing on the station-pair split and
  less than the closed form on the time split.

  WHAT THE FINDING DOES AND DOES NOT SUPPORT. This corpus is GENERATED by a
  mechanism that baseline 2 nearly reproduces in closed form, so a closed-form
  win here is close to the best case for the closed form and says nothing about
  what would happen on measured handover data, where the generating mechanism is
  unknown and the declared sequence is an approximation to it. The honest
  statement is the narrow one: WHEN the handover sequence is known and the
  source is close to constant-rate, the closed form is the better predictor and
  a forest fitted on a few dozen station configurations does not beat it. That
  is in README.md under Limitations and in MODEL_CARD.md.
