3  the quantile approximation this measurement CAUSED TO BE REPLACED
==============================================================================
  Baseline 1's conservative setting is evaluated at an upper quantile of the
  acquisition distribution. The first implementation obtained that quantile from
  the exact moments through a normal approximation, mean + z * sd, to avoid a
  Monte Carlo quantile inside a per-event loop. THIS CHECK FOUND THAT
  APPROXIMATION TO BE WRONG BY A FACTOR OF 2.03 and it was replaced, rather than
  the check being loosened to accommodate it. The replacement memoises the exact
  quantile over the 128 DISTINCT acquisition parameter tuples a corpus of 1536
  events carries, which costs about half a second for the whole scoring pass.

  The failure is structural and not a tuning matter. A geometric retry tail
  inflates the variance through a rare long attempt while leaving the 95th
  percentile inside the lognormal body, so a symmetric two-parameter
  approximation cannot track both ends. At a low abort probability it
  OVERSTATES; at a high one it understates. Both directions are below.
