A note on the unbounded cells. The simulation caps attempts at 200,
which is not the analytic unbounded process. At p = 0.75 the
probability of 200 consecutive failures is 0.75**200 = 1.029e-25, so the truncation bias is below the simulation's own
standard error by many orders of magnitude and is not what the
agreement is testing. At p = 0.95 it would not be, which is why the
sweep stops at 0.75.
