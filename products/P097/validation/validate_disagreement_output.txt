1. HAND-CALCULATED KNOWN ANSWERS
==============================================================================

Kendall tau-b, equation (1) of src/flakerank/disagreement.py:
  tau_b = (C - D) / sqrt((n0 - n1)(n0 - n2))
with n0 = n(n-1)/2 pairs, n1 and n2 the tied-pair counts of each vector.
Spearman rho is the Pearson correlation of midranks; with no ties that
reduces to 1 - 6 sum(d^2)/(n^3 - n), and with ties the shortcut is wrong
and the midrank form is the standard correction.

TIE HANDLING, stated because it is where two implementations of tau-b
can differ. A pair tied in either vector counts as neither concordant
nor discordant AND is removed from the denominator for that vector
separately, which is what makes this tau-b rather than tau-a. The
flake-rate vector here is heavily tied -- every test with no observed
failure has rate exactly 0, and there are 820 such pairs of 2556 -- so the correction is not
cosmetic on this data.
