The independently derived bracket on total wasted time -- P097 flakerank

The accumulator masks, groups and sums. The bounds here use a failure
COUNT plus statistics over the whole duration sample: two extreme order
statistics, and the second moment. Neither groups anything, and min and
max are selections rather than additions, so they cannot share an
accumulation error with the thing they bracket.

Equation (1): F * t_min <= W <= F * t_max, counting plus order statistics.
Equation (2): W <= sqrt(F * sum t^2), Cauchy-Schwarz against the all-ones
  vector; source Hardy, G. H., Littlewood, J. E. & Polya, G. (1952),
  Inequalities, 2nd edition, Cambridge University Press.
Equation (3): slack = SAFETY * F * eps * sum|t|, the standard forward
  error bound for floating-point summation; see Higham, N. J. (2002),
  Accuracy and Stability of Numerical Algorithms, 2nd edition, SIAM,
  chapter 4.

unit roundoff eps = 2**-53 = 1.1102230246251565e-16
SAFETY = 4.0, covering the grouped rather than strictly recursive
  accumulation order of numpy.add.at

WHAT THIS BOUND IS WORTH, stated rather than implied. The bracket's
width is set by the spread of the duration sample, and this history
spans 0.011 s to 1848.115 s. So:
  a DOUBLED accumulator total still fits inside it: True
  a TENFOLD total does not                        : False

The bound therefore catches structural errors -- the wrong mask, a
missing group, a sign, a double count -- and not every multiplicative
one. That is a property of the bound and not a defect in it, and the
control below uses a history where the bracket IS tight.
