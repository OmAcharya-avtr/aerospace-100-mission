ZernKit validation 1 -- orthonormality on the unit disc
========================================================================
Modes tested: Noll j = 1..36 (radial orders 0..7)
Reference: analytic Kronecker delta, Noll 1976 JOSA 66(3) Eq. 3

      40      128        2.442e-14          2.309e-14

     120      512        5.218e-14          5.115e-14

Note: B does not converge to machine precision. A masked square grid is
a first-order-accurate quadrature of a disc (the boundary is jagged), so
orthogonality is only approximate on sampled data. This is why
zernkit.fitting uses least squares rather than projection integrals.
