what this experiment supports
-----------------------------
1. The coverage-greedy reference reaches 0.993584 against 0.955457 for the
   learned gap finder, ahead on every one of the 60 seeds. If 2-way coverage
   is what you want, greedily maximising 2-way coverage is what to do, and no
   model is needed for it.
2. The learned gap finder and greedy max-min differ by +0.004204 in mean final
   coverage, with a paired standard deviation of 0.015119, and the learned model
   loses on 20 of 60 seeds. This experiment does NOT separate them.
3. On the occupancy metric the learned gap finder is -0.001273 against uniform
   random, losing on 30 of 60 seeds. On the metric closest to what the
   density model sees, it does not beat the null strategy.
4. Both non-trivial strategies beat uniform random (0.943215) on the
   2-way metric: max-min at 0.951254 and the learned model at 0.955457.
5. Cost: the learned gap finder spent 1.33 s against 0.03 s for
   greedy max-min on the same work, a factor of 40, for a difference this
   experiment cannot establish the sign of.
