3. unequal cell probabilities: the generalised expectation
----------------------------------------------------------
Equation (3) assumes equiprobable cells. Drawing a continuous parameter
uniformly over its declared interval does NOT make unequal-width bins
equiprobable, so the correct expectation is the sum over cells of
1 - (1 - p_c)^n, with p_c the product of the bin probabilities.
  bin probabilities, closing_speed: [0.081633, 0.102041, 0.204082, 0.612245]
  bin probabilities, range:         [0.078947, 0.131579, 0.263158, 0.526316]
     n    equiprob   generalised    measured    4 s.e.  verdict
    40     14.7895       11.0812     11.0968    0.0895  PASS
   120     15.9931       14.3867     14.3780    0.0662  PASS
   240     16.0000       15.5020     15.4972    0.0415  PASS
  The equiprobable column is the one that is wrong for this sampling
  rule, and it is wrong by roughly 3 cells in 16 at n = 240. The
  package reports the equiprobable reference because it is the only one
  computable without a model of how the campaign was sampled, and the
  README says so.
