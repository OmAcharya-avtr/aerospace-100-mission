E7  Findings, as measured
    1. On detumble time alone (w = 0) the learned scheduler beats the
       fixed gain: -0.206 [-0.308, -0.103] orbits, A WINS.
    2. At the default weight the advantage shrinks to -0.109 [-0.196, -0.021], A WINS.
    3. When energy is weighted heavily (w = 2) the ordering reverses: 0.181 [0.038, 0.324], B WINS.
    4. Against the three-coefficient power-law fit of the SAME training
       data the learned scheduler does not win at any weight tested: w=0 0.078 [0.010, 0.146] (B WINS), w=0.5 0.063 [-0.010, 0.135] (NOT RESOLVED).
    5. 100.0 % of the RandomForest's impurity importance sits on
       the two static vehicle features (dipole limit and inertia); the six
       time-varying magnetometer features carry 0.0 %. The
       'scheduler' is behaving as a static per-vehicle gain lookup, which
       is exactly what the power-law baseline already is.
