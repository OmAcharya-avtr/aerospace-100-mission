2.1 the grid
==============================================================================
All error figures are in milliradians. 'obs RMSE' is the root mean square over the cell of the NORM of the error inside the span of the floor-clearing degrees of freedom; 'CRB' is the MEDIAN over the cell of the total Cramer-Rao one sigma on that same subspace, sqrt(sum 1/lambda_i). 'obs/CRB' and 'MAP/CRB' are the MEDIAN of the PER-RECORD ratio of those two quantities, not a ratio of the two columns beside them: within one cell the bound varies by orders of magnitude between seeds because the excitation shares of (9) do, so a ratio of root-mean-squares is decided by the single worst-conditioned seed. For an efficient unbiased estimator the median per-record ratio is the median of a chi variate divided by sqrt of its degrees of freedom: 0.8326 on a rank-2 record and 0.8881 on a rank-3 one. 'unobs RMSE' is the per-component root mean square outside the retained span. 'z std' is the standard deviation of the standardised score (24) over the cell.

The per-record ratio is reported for information and is NOT the check. Its expected value depends on how the bound's mass is spread across directions: when all three contribute equally it is median(chi_3)/sqrt(3) = 0.8881, and when one ill-conditioned direction dominates the sum it falls to median(chi_1) = 0.6745. The near_single_axis cells are the second case. The check is on the standardised score of (24), one per retained degree of freedom, which is standard normal regardless of conditioning.

The Cramer-Rao bound of (12) constrains unbiased estimators only. The MAP estimator of (18) shrinks toward zero and is therefore biased, so a ratio below 1 is not a violation of anything. The price is the bias, measured below per profile as the mean projection of the error onto the truth direction, which is negative when the estimate is systematically short.
