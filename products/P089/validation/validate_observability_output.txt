==============================================================================
lambda_min is computed by numpy.linalg.eigh. It is bracketed below by Gershgorin's circle theorem and above by the Rayleigh quotient at the canonical basis vectors, neither of which uses an eigensolver. ==============================================================================
1.6 two rotation axes are enough for three alignment degrees of freedom
==============================================================================
By (10), lambda_i = (W/s^2)(1 - p_i), and a two-axis rotation has p = (1/2, 1/2, 0), so the third eigenvalue is the LARGEST. It is easy to assume three excited axes are needed; measured below, two suffice. The conditioning is reported beside it and the ordering is NOT the one a reader would guess, which is why it is measured and not asserted.

[PASS] both two-axis and three-axis records are well conditioned -- two-axis 2.0370, three-axis 1.6666, both against a declared bound of 10
  ORDERING, reported as measured and not as expected: the two-axis record is WORSE conditioned (2.0370) than the three-axis one (1.6666).
