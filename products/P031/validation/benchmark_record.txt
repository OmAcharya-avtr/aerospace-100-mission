CAVEAT
  These are host-side measurements of the harness, taken on a shared,
  single-core cloud container with four other build agents running
  concurrently. Absolute latencies are therefore pessimistic and their
  scatter is dominated by contention outside this process. They are not
  hardware numbers and must not be quoted as such.
  Level 4 validation is NOT claimed and is not reachable from this record:
  it requires this same harness run on a Jetson Orin Nano with its raw
  output captured, and until that exists the product is Level 3,
  hardware-pending.
