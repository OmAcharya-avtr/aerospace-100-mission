  INTERPRETATION
  The resolution term is negligible here because the clock is
  nanosecond-resolution; the scatter, which is contention on a
  shared single-core container, dominates by orders of magnitude.
  The term is carried anyway because on a coarse clock it would
  dominate instead, and a budget that drops it would be silently
  wrong there. The timing-call bias above is not negligible: it is
  a measurable fraction of a short iteration and is why this
  harness reports per-stage latency rather than claiming to measure
  the work alone.
