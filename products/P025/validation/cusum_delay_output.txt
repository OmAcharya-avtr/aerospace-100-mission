The m/S column is the price of the closed loop.  The Siegmund expression
assumes the residual mean steps to mu_ss at onset; it does not, because the
estimator absorbs part of the bias first, so the true delay is longer.  The
mean-path column, which runs the same CUSUM on the noise-free residual
profile, tracks the measurement far more closely.
