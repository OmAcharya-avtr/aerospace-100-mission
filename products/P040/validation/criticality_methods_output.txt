Reading of the result, stated plainly
==============================================================================
1. The exponent-bit heuristic does NOT win the headline metric at bit
   granularity: the learned predictor is ahead at every budget below the
   whole region. But the heuristic costs zero injections and zero training,
   and already reaches 87 % of the oracle at a 5 % budget, against the
   learned predictor's 98 % bought with 2816 injections and a forest fit.
   Per unit of effort the heuristic is far ahead, and for anyone who cannot
   run a ground-truth sweep it is the method to use.
2. At word granularity - which is what real ECC and TMR charge - the
   exponent heuristic is exactly uninformative. Its per-byte figure is
   constant at the random-selection expectation, because it gives every
   word the same score. The plain magnitude baseline beats the learned
   predictor there at 4 of the 6 budgets tested.
3. So which criticality method wins depends on the protection granularity,
   and no single method wins everywhere. That is the result; it has not
   been retuned to produce a cleaner one.
