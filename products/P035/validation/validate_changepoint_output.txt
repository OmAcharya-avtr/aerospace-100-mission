------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Series-wide false-alarm rate of binary segmentation
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The threshold is calibrated per segment.  Binary segmentation applies it at
every recursion level, so the probability of at least one spurious change
point in a pure-noise series can exceed the per-segment target.  How much it
exceeds it is an empirical question, measured here over 4000 pure-noise
series:

 min_segment  max_depth  any false CP        SE  mean count  inflation
          20         20       0.05150   0.00349      0.0540       1.03
          20          2       0.05150   0.00349      0.0540       1.03
          50         20       0.05150   0.00349      0.0527       1.03

Reported, not checked.  The measured inflation is modest, because the
top-level test gates the recursion: a series that fails to produce one
change point never reaches a second level, so the extra tests are not
independent of the first.  The mean number of spurious change points per
series exceeds the any-false-CP probability only slightly, which is the
same statement.  A user who needs a guaranteed series-wide rate should
calibrate the threshold against this measurement at their own segment
length, or use a penalised method such as PELT (Killick, Fearnhead and
Eckley 2012), which the `ruptures` package implements and this module does
not.
