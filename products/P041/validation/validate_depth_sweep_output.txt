HOW BIG IS THE REAL ERROR BAR? The FER SE column above is a binomial
standard error, which assumes codeword failures are independent. They are
not: they are driven by a handful of deep fades, so the binomial figure
understates the uncertainty. The honest error bar is the spread across
channel realisations, measured here over five seeds at the longest
correlation time, where the record holds the fewest fades.
