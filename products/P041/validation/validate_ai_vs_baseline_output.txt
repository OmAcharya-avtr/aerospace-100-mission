The memoryless closure of the level-crossing result over-predicts the
exceedance at t = MFD by the ratio above. Fade durations on this channel
are not exponentially distributed: short fades are far more common than an
exponential with the same mean. This is published as measured; equation
(16) is not retuned and not removed.

VERDICT
  The learned model improves on the best state-blind baseline, and the
  ROC AUC above 0.5 is the direct evidence that the state at the
  crossing carries information about the fade length. The size of the
  improvement over the empirical constant is the number to quote; the
  improvement over the exponential closure is mostly a calibration
  correction and overstates the case.

wrong, which is what makes the uncertainty usable for gating a depth
decision. A ratio near 1 means it is not, and that is reported as such.
