The error is dominated by the declared static asymmetry, which is exactly
what equation (5) of timetransfer.twoway says it must be: the estimate is
off by A/2 and no amount of data removes it. The reported uncertainty covers
it because the asymmetry was declared as a bound; with no asymmetry term the
same estimate carries a narrower interval, by the ratio printed in the next
section, and does not cover the error. That comparison is the product.
