3e. what the numbers mean, stated rather than left to the reader
==============================================================================
unaccounted    : measured 0.1200 against nominal 0.9500 -> optimistic
  An interval with no asymmetry term is a repeatability statement. It
  under-covers by the margin above, and that margin IS the asymmetry.
declared_bound : measured 0.9900 against nominal 0.9500 -> conservative
  A type-B bound at six times the generator's own asymmetry sd is honest and
  conservative. It is the right answer when external knowledge exists.
median_filter  : measured 0.9450 against nominal 0.9500 -> calibrated
learned_gbr    : measured 0.9525 against nominal 0.9500 -> calibrated
  Both corrected treatments use a residual spread measured on a split the
  corrector never saw. Where the verdict is not 'calibrated', the interval
  is wrong by the stated margin and README.md says so.

summary
==============================================================================
No coverage result is treated as a hard failure: this script measures the
calibration of the interval and the measurement is the deliverable. A
verdict other than 'calibrated' is reported in README.md, VALIDATION.md and
MODEL_CARD.md and is not retuned away.
