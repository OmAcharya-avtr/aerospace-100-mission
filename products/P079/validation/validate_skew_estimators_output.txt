4c. the Kalman consistency diagnostic, measured and reported
==============================================================================
The measurement variance R is taken from the data by a median-absolute-
deviation estimator with the Gaussian consistency factor 1/0.67449. The
queueing delay is a right-skewed gamma, so the MAD under-states its standard
deviation and the filter is expected to be over-confident. The measured
factor is below, and the implied inflation on the Kalman offset uncertainty
is its square root.
