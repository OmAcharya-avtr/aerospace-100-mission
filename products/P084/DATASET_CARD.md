# Dataset card — `transfergap` synthetic paired sim-to-field records

**Product:** P084 `transfergap` 0.1.0 · **Licence:** Apache-2.0 ·
© 2026 OPTIMA Organisation

**No measurement of any real aircraft, engine or test article appears anywhere
in this repository.** Every record is generated by committed code from a
declared seed.

## Source

`src/transfergap/synthetic.py`. Nothing is downloaded, nothing is committed as
data, and no file in this repository holds a record. Regeneration is
deterministic: `transfergap.synthetic.generate(n, campaign, seed)` returns the
same array for the same arguments on the measured environment, and
`tests/test_synthetic.py` asserts it.

Nothing over 1 MB is committed because nothing is committed at all; the
largest files in the repository are the six PNG figures the examples produce.

## What a record is

| field | type | unit | range |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `gaps` | `float64`, `(n,)` | kN.m | `[0, 40]` by equation (1) |
| `features[:, 0]` — Mach | `float64` | dimensionless | 0.55 to 0.95 |
| `features[:, 1]` — altitude | `float64` | km | 6.0 to 12.0 |
| `features[:, 2]` — angle of attack | `float64` | degrees | 0.0 to 9.0 |
| `features[:, 3]` — load factor | `float64` | dimensionless (g) | 0.8 to 3.0 |
| `unit` | `str` | — | `"kN.m"` |
| `bound_range` — `R` | `float` | kN.m | 40.0, declared |

The scenario: a digital twin of a transport-aircraft wing predicts wing-root
bending moment; a flight test measures the same quantity with a calibrated
strain channel whose full scale gives `R`.

## The generating process

```
e(x) = b(x) + s(x) Z,   Z ~ Normal(0, 1)                             (19)
b(x) = 2.0 + 6.0 sigmoid((M - 0.82)/0.05) + 3.0 (alpha/10)^2  [kN.m] (20)
s(x) = 1.5 + 4.0 sigmoid((M - 0.85)/0.04) + 1.0 (nz - 1)      [kN.m] (21)
d    = min(|e(x)|, 40.0)                                              (1)
```

`sigmoid(u) = 1/(1 + exp(-u))`. The conditional mean of `d`, neglecting the
clip, is the folded-normal mean of equation (22) — Leone, Nelson &
Nottingham (1961), *Technometrics* 3(4), 543–550, equation 2 — and is the
conditional truth the learned model is scored against.

**This is a declared fiction with an engineering shape, not a physical
derivation.** The growth is placed in the transonic corner because a steady
linear-aerodynamics twin loses fidelity as shock formation begins, and a
quadratic growth with angle of attack is added for the same kind of reason.
The specific constants were chosen so the mean discrepancy is a few per cent
of `R` and the upper tail reaches a substantial fraction of it, which is the
regime where the choice of bound matters. No constant here is taken from, or
claims to represent, any published measurement.

## The two campaign shapes

| campaign | how conditions are drawn | exchangeable in the index |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `random` | every feature independently and uniformly from the envelope | **yes**, by construction |
| `swept` | Mach swept monotonically across the index with a uniform jitter, other features independent | **no** — this is the deliberate counterexample |

The `swept` shape is how a flight-test card is actually flown: in order of
increasing Mach number. It is the alternative the exchangeability test in
`transfergap.exchangeability` is measured against, and the measured power of
that test against it is 0.4300 at n = 15 and 0.9967 at n = 120.

## The population truth

Measured by Monte Carlo on the `random` campaign, 400 000 draws, seed
20260101:

| quantity | value |
| --- | --- |
| `E[d]` | 5.119305 ± 0.006100 kN.m |
| 0.90 quantile | 10.376160 kN.m |
| 0.95 quantile | 12.782511 kN.m |
| 0.99 quantile | 17.670775 kN.m |
| `Var[d]` | 14.826382 kN.m² (200 000 draws, seed 4242) |
| clip fraction at `R = 40` | **0.000000** |

The Monte Carlo standard error is returned with the mean so that no comparison
against the truth is ever stated tighter than the truth is known.

## Splits

No fixed split is committed. Each validation script draws what it needs from
declared seeds:

| role | size | seed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| field campaign | 15, 25, 40 or 60 | `SEED_FIELD = 770000` plus a per-size and per-repetition offset |
| held-out scoring pool | 2500 (learned study), 4000 (bounds study) | `SEED_POOL = 880000`, 99000 |
| population truth | 400 000 | `SEED_TRUTH = 20260101` |
| conformal calibration | half of the field campaign | `SEED_MODEL = 990000` |

Campaigns and pools are drawn from independent seeds and never overlap.

## Limitations

- **Synthetic.** Nothing here establishes that any method in this package
  behaves the same way on a real campaign.
- **The error law is a folded normal** at every condition. A heavier tail would
  make every learned quantile worse and leave every distribution-free bound
  untouched, which is itself the argument for the distribution-free bound.
- **The conditional mean and scale are smooth** in Mach and angle of attack. A
  real twin can have a discontinuity at a configuration change.
- **Features are independent** in the `random` campaign. Real flight-test
  conditions are strongly correlated; Mach, altitude and load factor are not
  independently selectable.
- **Nothing is censored.** The clip fraction is 0.000000, so the censoring path
  of equation (1) is exercised only by the input-validation tests.
- **`R = 40 kN.m` is declared, not derived** from any calibration record.
- **One non-exchangeable alternative only**, and it is a monotone Mach sweep.
- **The truth is available.** That is the point of the dataset and the thing a
  real campaign never has, which is why the bounds in this package have to be
  distribution-free.

## Ethical considerations

No personal data of any kind. No proprietary or export-controlled data. No
real test data. The generator and every seed are in the repository, so any
number on any page of this product can be regenerated by a reader with a cold
clone.
