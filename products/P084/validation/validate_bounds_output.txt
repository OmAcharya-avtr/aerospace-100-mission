The distinction the two rate columns exist for: split conformal is
MARGINALLY valid, so its coverage column sits at the declared level while
its tolerance rate is near one half -- about half of campaigns produce a
limit that under-covers, and the guarantee is only that the average is
right. Wilks' limit is training-conditionally valid, so its tolerance rate
is the number that reaches the declared confidence. Reporting the coverage
column alone would make the two look equivalent.
