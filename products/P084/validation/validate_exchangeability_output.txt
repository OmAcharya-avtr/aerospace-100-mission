REPORTED AS FOUND. The power is not 1.0 at the sample sizes this product is
about, and that is the honest and useful number: at n = 15 the test misses a
genuinely non-exchangeable campaign most of the time. A programme with
fifteen paired points cannot establish that its conformal bound is valid,
and the distribution-free bound of equation (3), which needs no such
assumption, is the only answer available to it.
