defect 11 (criterion, this script): the pytest.approx absolute floor
==============================================================================
  pytest.approx(expected, rel=r) carries a DEFAULT absolute tolerance of
  1e-12 and accepts a value within EITHER bound. Every angle this
  package produces is of order 1e-05 rad, so that default dominates any
  relative tolerance tighter than 2e-08: an assertion written rel=1e-12
  is in fact a 2e-08 relative comparison.

  It mattered. total_rad is the angle between two unit vectors offset
  either side of the line of sight, so it is 2 arctan(theta / 2) =
  theta - theta^3 / 12 and NOT theta. The known-answer test asserted it
  equal to theta at rel=1e-12 and passed.

  theta = 2 v_perp / c                : 5.003461427972281e-05 rad
  2 arctan(theta / 2)                 : 5.0034614269284495e-05 rad
  total_rad                           : 5.0034614269284495e-05 rad
  relative deficit                    : 2.086218195129917e-10
  theta^2 / 12                        : 2.0862188551005347e-10
  ratio of the two                    : 0.999999684

  Re-running the whole suite with every rel= call given abs=0.0 found
  exactly three assertions of 271 that depended on the default. All
  three are now explicit and the whole suite passes with abs=0.0
  everywhere.
  [PASS] total_rad equals 2 arctan(theta / 2) to rounding
  [PASS] the relative deficit is theta^2 / 12 -- measured 2.086218195e-10, predicted 2.086218855e-10
