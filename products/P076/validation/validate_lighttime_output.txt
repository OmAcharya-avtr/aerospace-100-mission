4. contraction factor against the remote terminal's radial speed over c
==============================================================================
  The fixed-point map is tau -> |r_remote(t +/- tau) - r_local| / c with
  r_local HELD FIXED, because the point-ahead angle is defined at one event
  at the local terminal. Its derivative is therefore +/- (v_remote . n) / c
  and NOT (v_rel . n) / c: the contraction is driven by the remote
  terminal's radial velocity in the frame the states are given in, not by
  the relative radial velocity. Both candidates are printed, because the
  first version of this check used the relative one and the measurement
  disagreed with it by a factor of two on one geometry and by twelve orders
  of magnitude on another.

6. the gap between the two light times
==============================================================================
  tau_tx - tau_rx = 2 (v_remote . n) tau / c to first order, for the same
  reason as section 4. The leo_crossing and leo_trailing geometries share a
  local terminal and a range and differ only in the remote plane, and their
  remote radial velocities happen to be equal, so their light-time gaps are
  equal too even though their RELATIVE radial velocities are 3736 m/s and
  zero. That coincidence is what exposed the wrong predictor.
