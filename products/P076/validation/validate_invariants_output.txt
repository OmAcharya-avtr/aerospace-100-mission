3. rotational equivariance of the whole configuration
==============================================================================
  Rotating both terminal states must leave every angle unchanged and must
  rotate the offset 3-vector with the configuration.

  The acceptance criterion is ABSOLUTE and is the float64 direction
  resolution of each geometry, pointahead.budget.direction_noise_floor_rad.
  It was first written as a pure relative tolerance of 1e-12 and that
  failed, at a worst relative residual of 3.7e-09 on a crosslink whose
  point-ahead angle is 2.0e-07 rad: a residual of 7.7e-16 rad is 3.7e-09 of
  an angle that small, and 7.7e-16 rad is three hundred times BELOW the
  2.3e-13 rad resolution of that geometry. A relative criterion on a
  quantity whose scale varies by two orders across the family is the wrong
  criterion, and both residuals are printed below so a reader can see that.

  crosslinks                         : 120
  smallest point-ahead angle         : 2.045329e-07 rad
  worst absolute angle residual      : 7.532847e-15 rad
  worst relative angle residual      : 3.682951e-08
  worst absolute offset residual     : 7.740676e-15 rad
  worst relative offset residual     : 3.784562e-08
  residuals above their own floor    : 0 of 240
  [PASS] every rotation residual is below the float64 resolution of its geometry -- 0 of 240 residuals exceeded their floor

==============================================================================
5. linearity in the transverse relative speed
==============================================================================
  The angle is 2 v_perp / c, so scaling the transverse relative velocity
  scales the angle by the same factor. Measured over seven decades.

  The criterion is absolute-or-relative, whichever is looser: a relative
  tolerance of 1e-09 OR the float64 direction resolution of the geometry.
  A pure 1e-09 relative criterion failed at v_perp = 1e-03 m/s, where the
  angle is 6.7e-12 rad and the residual is 3.3e-17 rad -- three hundred
  times below the 1.0e-14 rad resolution of the geometry, and therefore
  not a measurement of the code at all.
