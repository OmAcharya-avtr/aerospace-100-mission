3  the verdict
==============================================================================
  At duty 1.00 the die rose 10.94 degC over 1644 samples
  and the mean CPU clock stayed at 1728000.0 kHz throughout.

  Consequences, both of which the product states where the figures appear:
  - This is the most direct evidence in the campaign that the board did not
    thermally throttle under the declared payload at MAXN_SUPER.
  - Baseline 2's clock-to-throughput chain therefore has no thermal signal
    to act on, which is why baseline 2 reduces to baseline 1 rather than
    improving on it. That reduction is a measured property of this board
    under this payload, not a defect in the implementation.
