8b  an independent bound on the MEASURED asymptote
==============================================================================
  The mission's build guide requires at least one check against a bound derived from mathematics the implementation does not use. On SYNTHETIC fixtures that is validate_independent_bound.py, where the generating model IS first order and the bound is a correctness check on the estimator. Here the same bound is applied to the BOARD, where it tests something different: whether a first-order model describes this system. Those are not the same question and the outcomes are reported differently -- an estimator that misses its bound is a defect, a board that misses it is data.

  [FINDING] monotonicity. A monotone first-order rise approaches its asymptote from below, so the fitted asymptote should exceed the final-window mean. MEASURED: asymptote 56.765 degC against final-window mean 56.999 degC, a shortfall of +0.233 degC. The bound is NOT satisfied on this board.

  [FINDING] the local-slope identity T_inf = T + tau dT/dt, with tau taken from the COOLDOWN traces (79.70 to 153.43 s) and the final-window slope +0.000353 +/- 0.000256 degC/s at 4 sigma. Different runs, the opposite direction of heat flow and a different fit, so this shares no data and no algebra with the rise fit.
            bracket [56.803, 57.117] degC, width 0.315 degC. MEASURED asymptote 56.765 degC lies OUTSIDE, BELOW it, by 0.038 degC.
            The bracket's width is slope uncertainty times the cooldown tau spread, so the slack follows the CONDITIONING of this bound and not a constant.
[PASS] the disagreement with the independent bracket is smaller than the fit's own rms residual -- 0.0378 degC against rms residual 0.2100 degC, a ratio of 0.180. A disagreement LARGER than the fit's own residual scale would be an implementation defect; one smaller is the model's known inadequacy showing up where it should
[PASS] control: an asymptote displaced beyond the measured bracket is caught (must fire) -- FIRED; a corrupted asymptote of 58.709 degC is outside
[PASS] control: the bracket does NOT admit an arbitrary value (must NOT fire) -- did not fire; an asymptote 10 degC low is outside, so the bracket has content

  WHAT THESE TWO FINDINGS MEAN, stated rather than left to the reader:
  The single-exponential fit UNDER-predicts the steady-state die temperature of this board. Both independent bounds say the true asymptote is above 56.765 degC, and the corroborating evidence is in the fit's own residuals: lag-1 autocorrelation +0.732 and rms residual 0.2100 degC over 8.09 tau. Residuals that autocorrelated are the signature of a SECOND, SLOWER time constant that the single exponential cannot represent, which is exactly what a die, a heat spreader and a fan-cooled heatsink should produce. The measured final-window slope is still +0.000353 degC/s at the end of a 720 s soak: the die had not finished rising, it had only slowed to within the declared equilibrium criterion.
  This is a measured property of the board and of the first-order model, not a defect in the implementation, and it is NOT reported as a failed check. What would be a defect is a disagreement larger than the fit's own residual scale, and that is the check above.
  Consequence for the product: baseline 2's steady-state die prediction 56.649 degC is -0.0597 degC against the measured 56.7085 degC, which is inside the run-to-run half-width 0.2583 degC. The model is good enough for the use it is put to here and is biased low in a known direction; a longer soak would widen that bias.
