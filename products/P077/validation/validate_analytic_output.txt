check 2: reported truncation bound against the measured truncation
==============================================================================
The bound is n * p**K / (1 - p) from Bernoulli's inequality, so it is an
upper bound on everything the truncated series leaves out and must never be
smaller than the measured change. The slack is the bound minus the measured
truncation and must be non-negative up to the rounding of the subtraction
itself, which is of order eps times the sum; the comparison tolerance is
16 * eps * E[R] and is stated per row.

This check found two defects on its first two runs and both are recorded
here rather than in a commit message.

  1. The first run compared the slack against a flat 1e-18 and reported
     four of nine rows as failures at a slack of -1e-16. That is the
     subtraction's own rounding, not a violated bound, and the comparison
     was wrong rather than the bound.
  2. With the tolerance corrected, one row still failed: n = 1000, p = 0.3,
     slack -1.83e-13 against a tolerance of 2.39e-14, eight times too large
     to be the subtraction. It traced to the series term itself. Written as
     1 - (1 - p**k)**n, a small p**k rounds with an absolute error of half
     an epsilon, the n-th power multiplies the relative error by n, and the
     final subtraction from 1 leaves an absolute error of order n * eps,
     which at n = 1000 is 2e-13 -- a thousand times the stated truncation
     tolerance of the series. The term is now evaluated as
     -expm1(n * log1p(-p**k)), which is the same function and not the same
     computation, and the worst slack over the nine pairs moved from
     -1.83e-13 to +7.30e-16. relfiledelivery.analytic.rounds_cdf was
     changed the same way.
