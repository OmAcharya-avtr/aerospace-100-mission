==============================================================================
is the mean a description of the distribution
==============================================================================
For each scenario: the fraction of completed runs landing within plus or
minus five per cent of the mean, and within plus or minus one per cent. If
the mean described the distribution those fractions would be substantial.
The density of a unimodal distribution near its own mean is high; the
density of a round-quantised or contact-quantised one can be zero.

                        scenario     mean s   median s  mean/med  within 1%  within 5%  max/med
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
          no outages, continuous     4.5373     5.1676    0.8780     0.0000     0.0000   2.2978
  correlated outages, continuous     7.0532     6.0142    1.1728     0.0240     0.1340   4.8386
  correlated outages, 6 contacts    12.8544    11.6930    1.0993     0.0177     0.1571   4.5144

A uniform distribution over the same support would put about 2 per cent of
its mass in a plus-or-minus-one-per-cent band around its mean, so a figure
far below that is the quantitative version of the claim.

==============================================================================
per-contact completion probability
==============================================================================
The answer to the operator's actual question. A single expected delivery
time cannot express it: what is wanted is the probability the file is down
by the end of pass k, for each k, and that is a vector.

            mode   by pass 0   by pass 1   by pass 2   by pass 3   by pass 4   by pass 5
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  unacknowledged      0.0010      0.0010      0.0010      0.0010      0.0010      0.0010
    deferred_nak      0.2637      0.7873      0.9353      0.9870      0.9973      0.9997
   immediate_nak      0.2590      0.7467      0.9270      0.9823      0.9967      0.9993

Unacknowledged mode is in the table because its row is the point: with no
retransmission the completion probability does not improve with more passes.

The floor is the lossless overhead of the declared segmentation, which no
run can beat. On a windowed link a truncated run can fall below the floor of
a COMPLETED transfer, because it stopped early; those runs are counted in the
completion fraction and not in the delivery-time distribution.
