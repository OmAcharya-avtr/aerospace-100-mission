==============================================================================
what this check is, and what it is not
==============================================================================
It is the only check in this repository whose reference is not another
computation of the sensitivity matrix. The bound comes from the Fisher
information; the spread comes from an estimator that never sees it.

It is NOT a check that the estimator is efficient. A spread far ABOVE the
bound would pass this check and would mean the estimator is wasteful; the
ratios are printed so a reader can see which it is. On this plant the
ratios sit close to one, which is what a maximum-likelihood estimator on a
nearly linear problem should do, and it is reported rather than asserted,
because the Cramer-Rao inequality does not promise it.
