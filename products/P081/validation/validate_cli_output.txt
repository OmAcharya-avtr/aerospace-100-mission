3. this script pollutes nothing, and nothing polluting is tracked
==============================================================================
Two distinct claims, because they have two distinct owners.

An earlier revision of this check, in another product of this mission,
asserted that NO cache directory and no bytecode file existed anywhere in
the tree at the moment it ran. That claim is not a product's to make and it
is unsatisfiable under the release gate: the gate runs `ruff check src/
tests/` with its working directory set to the product root as check 2,
which writes `.ruff_cache` into the tree, and then re-executes the
validation scripts as check 6. No change to the product could have passed.

The two claims below are what this product can be held to, and both are
stricter than the old one in the direction that matters: the old check said
nothing at all about what is TRACKED, which is the only thing a reader with
a cold clone ever sees.
