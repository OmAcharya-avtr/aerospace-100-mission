NOTES / KNOWN DISCREPANCIES:
  - HV5/7: r0 = 4.962 cm is below the 5 cm floor - HV5/7 is *defined* to give 5 cm, i.e. it sits exactly on the floor of the band; the 0.75 % shortfall is the model's own arithmetic, not a band excursion. Reported, not tuned.
  - SLC-Day: r0 = 4.339 cm is below the 5 cm floor - a daytime sea-level model dominated by surface convection - a stronger regime than the good-astronomical-site statistics the band describes, so a value below the floor is the expected result, not an error. Reported, not tuned.
  - Bufton rms wind over 5-20 km evaluates to 22.9637 m/s for v_g = 5 m/s, not the 21 m/s pseudowind of HV 5/7. The convention behind the literature's 21 m/s (band limits, treatment of the ground term, or an added slew-rate term) could not be established in this build. The 21 m/s value is retained in hv57() because it is the published model parameter; the mismatch is reported here.
