4. what the numbers mean, stated plainly
==============================================================================
  Against the stronger baseline, a per-command lint with a timing pass misses
  0.4250 of the violations a forward walk finds on this population,
  and 0.4418 of the violations that only a sequence-aware check can
  reach. It also emits more findings than there are violations: its
  false-positive rate against the forward walk is 0.9371, because it
  evaluates every occurrence of every command against the declared initial
  state and so re-reports a condition that an earlier command already fixed.

  Both numbers are statements about this generator and this defect
  population. They are not estimates of how often a real mission's own
  checking script misses a real defect, and nothing here measures that.
