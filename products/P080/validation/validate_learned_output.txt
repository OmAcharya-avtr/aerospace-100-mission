mean weight on the most recent difference  : -0.3165
A negative weight here says the quantised first differences of this signal
set are anti-correlated on average, so the least-squares optimum sits
between 'previous' (weight 0) and the reflection of 'linear' (weight 1).
That is a statement about this generator's noise, not about telemetry.
