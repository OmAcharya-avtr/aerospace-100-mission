==============================================================================
the first-order entropy reference is not a floor for a coder with memory
==============================================================================
For the package's own coder the first-order entropy IS effectively a floor,
and that is a property of the coder and not of information theory: static
order-0 rANS codes each symbol independently against one shipped table, so
it cannot beat the memoryless entropy of its own symbol stream except by
the slack in the table quantisation. A coder with memory is under no such
constraint. The column below compares the lzma and bz2 payloads over the
SAME residual stream against the entropy of that stream: where the ratio
is below 1, a real coder has gone below the 'floor'.

Two entropy columns, because the difference matters. symbol_H is the figure
the certificate carries: first-order entropy over the 256-symbol alphabet,
in which every large residual has been collapsed onto one escape symbol. On
an escape-heavy stream it understates by orders of magnitude -- white_noise
below is the extreme case. value_H has no escape and is the figure to
compare against a byte count.

Sibling product P072 of this portfolio measured 0.9978 of its held-out
cases compressing below their measured first-order entropy. The number
here is lower because these residual streams are already decorrelated by
the predictor, which is what the entropy figure is computed on, and because
this product charges the header to the rate and P072 compared ratios.
Neither number licenses treating the entropy as a lower bound on the
achievable rate of a coder with memory.
