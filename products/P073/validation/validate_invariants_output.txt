candidate grid gives ten placements of a 200 s contact and every corruption
below stays inside the window. That matters: a corruption that also moved a
contact out of its window would be caught by the containment check instead of
by the one under test.

The two rows expecting False are the ones that make the rest meaningful. A
checker that fires on everything is not a checker, and a control built on an
offset that merely reaches the turnaround would be one of those rows, not a
violation.
