  240 s on flat/polar: -2

Steps safe on every case measured: 30 s, 45 s, 60 s, 120 s
The default in contactsched.visibility.compute_windows is 30 s and the default
in contactsched.dataset.generate_instance is 45 s. Both are in that list. A
step is safe for THESE orbits and masks and nothing more general is claimed:
any sampled root search can miss a pass shorter than its step, and the loss
measured above at 240 s is exactly that happening.
