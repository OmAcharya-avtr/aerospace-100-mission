THE RESULT, STATED AS MEASURED:
  at a 1 per cent false-positive rate on the training regime the learned model reaches 0.99284 recall against 0.97733 for the one-line closed form, 0.30112 for miss-distance thresholding and 0.03436 for the Mahalanobis threshold.
  on the homogeneous regime the closed form reaches 1.00000 and the learned model 0.98840: the closed form wins.
  on the dilution regime, never trained on, the closed form reaches 1.00000 and the learned model 0.99784: the closed form wins again.
  the product specification predicted that a Mahalanobis threshold would match the learned model. On the broad nominal population it does not: it is the worst of the three analytic screens at 0.03436. The reason is measurable and is measured: the Mahalanobis distance is invariant to R / sigma_eff, which spans the decades shown in check 1 on the nominal regime and less than a factor of two on the homogeneous one, where the same screen recovers to 0.83720. The prediction holds under a condition the specification did not state, and the condition is the spread of R / sigma_eff in the population.
  the learned model is therefore ahead on one regime of three, by +0.01551 recall, and behind on two. No screen was retuned after this was measured.

THIS CHECK FAILS AND IS LEFT FAILING. The forest is under-confident at high
predicted probability and over-confident in the middle bins, which is the
usual behaviour of an averaged tree ensemble. It is not repaired because the
product's figure of merit, recall at a fixed false-positive rate, depends
only on the ordering of the scores and is invariant under any strictly
monotone recalibration: an isotonic or Platt fit would move the Brier score
and this table and would not move a single recall number in check 2. The
consequence for a user is stated in MODEL_CARD.md: the predicted probability
is usable as a ranking and is not usable as a probability.
