[C7] Honest limitations of this experiment
  1. One realisation of one synthetic channel. The AR(1) lognormal model
     has the right first-order distribution and an exponential
     autocorrelation, and neither the measured scintillation spectrum nor
     aperture averaging, beam wander or pointing jitter.
  2. The analytic predictor is fitted to the same model family that
     generated the data. On a real record it would be fitting an
     approximation, and its advantage would shrink.
  3. Rows overlap heavily, so the effective sample size is the number of
     independent onsets, a few hundred on the test split. Brier
     differences in the fourth decimal place are not resolvable.
  4. No model here is calibrated across turbulence conditions: the
     channel parameters are stationary over the whole record, which a
     real link's are not over four seconds, let alone four hours.

  Compute: 2 cores, 7.8 GiB shared. Longest single fit: the 200-tree
  forest on 47993 rows.
