  The fitted chain reproduces the MEAN dwell almost exactly -- it is
  fitted to do so -- and gets the DISPERSION wrong by a factor of about
  2.7. The quantised state of a continuous Gauss-Markov process is not
  Markov, because 'below the threshold' discards how far below. A fade
  one sample old sits at the threshold and usually recovers at once; a
  fade one correlation length old has gone deeper and lasts longer.
