=== jitterscope anomaly detector benchmark ===
records: 60 s @ 1000 Hz; windows 1.0 s, 50% overlap; 24 log-spaced PSD bins (1 Hz - Nyquist)
training: 4 nominal records, seeds [1000, 1001, 1002, 1003]
test set: 6 records each of nominal / new_tone / band_shift / transient (seeds 2000+)
threshold rule (both models): q0.995 of held-out nominal scores

training feature matrix: (476, 24) (windows x bins)
baseline fit: 0.001 s, threshold = 3.9906 (max |z|)
MLP fit:      2.452 s, threshold = 1.0099 (reconstruction MSE)
MLP architecture: MLPRegressor(24 -> 16 -> 6 -> 16 -> 24), tanh, adam, alpha=1e-3, max_iter=3000, random_state=0

test windows: 2856 total, 952 faulty, 1904 nominal

--- Verdict ---
The CLASSICAL BASELINE WINS on F1: 0.9644 vs 0.9600 for the MLP (delta +0.0044).
ROC AUC: baseline 0.9797, MLP 0.9784 (baseline better by 0.0013).
Interpretation: the synthetic faults are additive-energy signatures that raise
log-PSD in specific bins, which is exactly the alternative hypothesis the z-score
baseline is built for. The MLP has no structural advantage on this dataset and
carries extra fit cost and hyperparameters. It is retained for the case of
correlated multi-band shape changes that a per-bin marginal test cannot see;
that case is NOT demonstrated here.

total benchmark runtime: 6.4 s on 2 CPU cores
