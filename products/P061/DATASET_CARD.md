# Dataset card — activityplan synthetic activity dataset

**Fully synthetic. No real spacecraft, instrument or mission data is used
anywhere in this repository, and no constant in the generator was identified
from a vehicle.**

## What it contains

Rows are **activity requests** with a realised duration and energy:

| field | unit | range | role |
|---|---|---|---|
| `activity_type` | — | one of `image`, `spectro`, `radar`, `calibration` | feature, one-hot |
| `slew_angle_deg` | deg | 0–90 (0–70 in planning instances) | feature |
| `data_mbit` | Mbit | 10–800 (40–420 in planning instances) | feature, and a declared plan input |
| `instrument_temperature_c` | C | -30–35 | feature |
| `gain` | dimensionless | 0.6–1.8 | feature |
| `analytic_duration_s` | s | derived | feature and baseline prediction |
| `analytic_mean_power_w` | W | derived | feature and baseline prediction |
| `duration_s` | s | realised | **target 1** |
| `energy_wh` | W\*h | realised | **target 2** |
| `priority` | dimensionless | one of 1, 2, 3, 5 | plan input, not a model feature |

## Source

Generated by committed, seeded code. Nothing is downloaded and no data file is
committed, because regeneration is deterministic and costs under a second per
thousand rows:

```python
import numpy as np
from activityplan.features import feature_matrix, realise, sample_requests

rng = np.random.default_rng(61)
requests = sample_requests(1400, rng)
realised = [realise(request, rng) for request in requests]
x = feature_matrix(list(requests))
duration_s = np.array([item.duration_s for item in realised])
energy_wh = np.array([item.energy_wh for item in realised])
```

Dataset sizes used by the committed numbers: **1400 activities per seed** for
`validate_predictor.py` (seeds 61–65), of which 1000 are fitted and 400 held
out; **1000** for `validate_warmstart.py`, `validate_robust.py` and
`worked_example.py`, all at seed 61.

## The generative process, declared in full

`activityplan.features.realise`. With `dT = max(0, 10 - instrument_temperature_c)`
in degrees C:

```
duration = analytic_duration
         + cold_settle_s_per_10c * dT / 10
         + gain_penalty_s_per_mbit * (gain - 1)^2 * data_mbit
         + Normal(0, 0.08 * analytic_duration)        floored at 15 s

power    = analytic_power
         + heater_w_per_10c * dT / 10
         + Normal(0, 0.03 * analytic_power)           floored at 5 W

energy   = power * duration / 3600
```

Per-type constants are in `features.TYPE_CONSTANTS`, with their units in the
comment block above the table.

**The first two added terms and the third are exactly what the analytic baseline
omits.** That is the whole reason the learned model wins, and it is declared here
rather than left to be discovered.

**The duration noise is heteroscedastic by design**: its standard deviation
scales with the analytic duration. That is the condition under which a
constant-width conformal interval attains marginal coverage while failing
conditional coverage, which is the measurement in
`validation/validate_predictor_output.txt` check 4.

## Splits

| split | size (per seed, predictor runs) | how |
|---|---:|---|
| proper training | 650 | seeded permutation of the first 1000 rows, 65 % |
| calibration | 350 | the remaining 35 %, disjoint |
| held out | 400 | rows 1001–1400, never seen by either forest or the calibration step |

Disjointness between the training and calibration parts is what the split
conformal guarantee rests on, and it is enforced by
`DurationEnergyPredictor.fit` rather than left to the caller.

## Known limitations

1. **It is synthetic.** Nothing in it was measured. The shapes of the analytic
   terms are standard timeline arithmetic (Wertz & Larson 1999, chapters 2 and
   14); the values are declared constants of a benchmark.
2. **Features are sampled independently.** On a real mission instrument
   temperature correlates with orbital position, pointing correlates with target
   geometry and data volume correlates with activity type beyond what the type
   constants capture. This is the dataset's main unrealism.
3. **No temporal structure.** Activities are exchangeable draws with no ordering,
   no seasonal effect and no ageing, which is precisely the condition split
   conformal needs and precisely what real operations data will not satisfy.
4. **No distribution shift and no outliers.** There is no mechanism in the
   generator for an anomalous activity, so nothing here measures how the model
   behaves on one.
5. **The floors bite.** Duration is floored at 15 s and power at 5 W. For the
   declared sampling ranges those floors are reached only for extreme noise
   draws, but they make the noise distribution not exactly normal in the tail.
6. **Priority is not a model feature.** It is a plan input drawn uniformly from
   {1, 2, 3, 5}. A real tasking system's priorities would correlate with activity
   type and data volume; here they do not.

## Intended use

Benchmarking the learned predictor against the analytic baseline, measuring
conformal interval coverage, and measuring the downstream effect of prediction
error on a plan. **Not for training anything intended to fly, and not for any
claim about real activity durations.**
