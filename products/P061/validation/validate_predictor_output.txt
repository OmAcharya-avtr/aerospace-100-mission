check 2: the irreducible noise floor, so the win is readable
==============================================================================
duration closed form: the generator adds Normal(0, 0.08 * analytic_duration), and
the mean absolute deviation of a centred normal is sigma * sqrt(2/pi), so the
best achievable MAE for any predictor of the duration is the mean of that over
the test set. The Monte Carlo estimate repeats the generator 200 times per
request and scores against the per-request sample mean, which also covers the
floor applied at 15 s and the energy target, where the noise enters as a product.
