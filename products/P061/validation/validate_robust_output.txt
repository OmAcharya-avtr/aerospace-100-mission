==============================================================================
check 5: why duration robustness is ANTI-robust for the recorder
==============================================================================
The result above is not what a reader would expect, so here is the mechanism,
measured. An activity's data volume is DECLARED, not predicted. Its production
rate is therefore data_mbit / duration_s, which is INVERSELY proportional to the
duration. Booking the conformal upper bound on the duration makes the plan
assume a LOW production rate; the realised duration is shorter, so the real rate
is HIGHER, and the recorder fills faster than the plan allowed for.

Planning against an upper bound on duration is conservative for time and for
energy and anti-conservative for the recorder. A duration-robust plan is not a
resource-robust plan.

mode         planned rate  realised rate  rate ratio  frac rate underbooked
oracle            0.93753        0.93753      1.0000                 0.0000
analytic          0.99237        0.93753      0.9473                 0.2381
ml-point          0.94028        0.93753      1.0032                 0.5060
ml-robust         0.79408        0.93753      1.1911                 0.9940

rates are Mbit/s. A rate ratio above 1 means the realised production outran the
plan's assumption. ml-robust has the largest ratio of the three achievable modes,
which is the quantitative statement of the mechanism above, and it is why
ml-robust fails recorder-capacity more often than ml-point does.

==============================================================================
check 6: the analytic duration model's own bias, for reference
==============================================================================
analytic duration mean signed error : -16.7371 s
analytic duration fraction optimistic: 0.6783
the analytic model is optimistic on average because the generator's two omitted
terms are both non-negative, which is why its plans fail more often than the
learned ones when they fail.

Taken with check 5, the two failure mechanisms are opposite: the analytic model
underbooks DURATION and breaks the battery, while the duration-robust plan
underbooks the data RATE and breaks the recorder. Neither is fixed by a better
duration predictor alone, and this package does not claim to fix either.
