--- 3b. Independent cross-check of the total variance ---
The piston-removed variance over a circular aperture can also be
obtained without Zernikes at all:
    sigma^2 = (1/2) <D_phi(|r1 - r2|)> over two independent points
with the analytic separation density of a disc,
    f(s) = (2 s / R^2)(2/pi)[arccos(s/2R) - (s/2R) sqrt(1 - (s/2R)^2)].
separation density normalisation : 0.999999999995  (must be 1)
structure-function route         : 1.032422 (D/r0)^(5/3)
Zernike-variance-sum route       : 1.032765 (D/r0)^(5/3)
difference between the two routes: 0.033%
Noll (1976) published Delta_1    : 1.029900 (D/r0)^(5/3)
both routes exceed the published value by 0.24% and 0.28%.
Reported, not tuned away: two independent derivations inside this
package agree with each other to 0.04% and sit ~0.25% above Noll's
published third significant figure.
result (routes agree to 0.1%)    : PASS

Honest finding: near J = 21, where the asymptote is customarily used,
it agrees with the exact sum to 1.2%. At larger J the two drift apart:
the exact sum falls with a local log-slope tending to 0.834, close to
the 5/6 = 0.8333 implied by mode counting (J ~ n^2/2, per-order
variance ~ n^(-8/3)), whereas Noll's asymptote uses sqrt(3)/2 = 0.8660.
This package uses the exact sum everywhere; the asymptote is provided
as published reference data only.
