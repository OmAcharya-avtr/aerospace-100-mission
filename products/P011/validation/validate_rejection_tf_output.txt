A sinusoidal tilt of unit RMS is injected in place of the atmosphere;
the residual RMS is compared with the same analytic |E(z)|.

First, the modelling floor. The scalar model assumes the sensor and
mirror reproduce the disturbance exactly. They do not: measuring a
unit-RMS tilt, reconstructing it and putting it on the mirror in one
perfect step leaves a residual, and no closed-loop measurement can
agree with the scalar model better than that. It is measured here
rather than guessed, and it sets the tolerance for this check.
one-shot sensor + DM tilt reproduction residual : 0.0224 (2.24% of the input)
