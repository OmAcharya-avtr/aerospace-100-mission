The Fourier screen is short of the continuous theory at every
separation: it carries no power below 1/(N d) or above the grid
Nyquist. The deficit grows with separation because the missing power
is at low frequency. This is the method, not the implementation.

Subharmonics recover a large part of the deficit and the recovery is
strongest at the largest separations, which is what they are for. The
remaining shortfall at small r is the high-frequency truncation at the
grid Nyquist and cannot be fixed by subharmonics.
