5. THE SEED AXIS IS A DIFFERENT AXIS
==============================================================================
a quantity that is a deterministic function of the seed yields the same
value on every repeat at a fixed seed, so repeats carry ZERO information
about seed-dependence and the Bernoulli formulae above do not apply.
seeds drawn without replacement from a finite declared set give a

7. WHAT THE BOUND ASSUMES, and the direction the assumption fails in
==============================================================================
independence across repeats is the strong assumption. Repeats inside one
process share RNG state, import caches, a fixed PYTHONHASHSEED and a warm
filesystem cache, so they are POSITIVELY CORRELATED, the true detection
power is LOWER than the formula, and p_max is OPTIMISTIC. The mitigation
is a fresh subprocess in a fresh pristine copy with a fresh hash seed,
which seedaudit.dynamic does. The residual correlation is not zero and
this product does not estimate it.
