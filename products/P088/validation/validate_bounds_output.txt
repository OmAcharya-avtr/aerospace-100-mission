where bound 3 is loose, and by how much
==============================================================================
Bound 3 counts identical-signature pairs in the removed and added pools. It
is an upper bound on perfect-structural matches and nothing stops it being
far above the realised count: the matcher also has to clear the threshold on
the combined score, which carries a name term. The slack is reported rather
than presented as a tight result.

size  candidate pairs  identical signatures  accepted perfect  slack
----  ---------------  --------------------  ----------------  -----
30    36               3                     3                 0
80    121              8                     8                 0
200   529              20                    20                0

Measured outcome: the slack is zero at every swept size, so on this generator
the bound is tight rather than loose. Every injected rename preserves the
structural signature exactly -- the generator changes only the name -- and
every such pair also clears the combined threshold. A generator that also
perturbed a field across the rename would open the slack up, and the bound
would still hold.
