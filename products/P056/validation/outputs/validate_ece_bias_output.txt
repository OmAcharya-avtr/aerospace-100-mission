Structural reason, and the refined statement that replaces the naive one.
Two biases act in opposite directions. Bernoulli noise in obar_k biases the
estimate UP by about 0.32 sqrt(B/n). Binning averages real miscalibration
inside a bin, which biases the estimate DOWN by an amount set by the bin
width and the curvature of the calibration map, and which does not shrink
with n. At n = 1000 the noise term dominates at every bin count here. At
n = 20000 with only 15 bins the two are comparable and the net bias is
slightly negative. So 'ECE is biased upward' is not a safe statement: it is
the small-sample statement, and the crossing point depends on the bin count.

redrawing outcomes from the forecasts themselves. On a miscalibrated
forecaster that null is the wrong null: it describes a *calibrated*
forecaster with the same forecast distribution, whose estimator noise is of
the same size as the raw estimate's noise but whose population ECE is 0. The
subtraction therefore removes most of the real miscalibration along with the
bias, and the measured damage reaches 57.9x the raw error.
Consequence for use, stated in README.md and MODEL_CARD.md: read
DebiasedECE.p_value first. A large p-value means the calibrated null is
tenable and the debiased value is the one to quote. A small p-value means it
is not, and the raw ECE with its null interval is what to report.
