What this measures
------------------
From the import text alone, a single-module change on this repository
selects a mean 0.4015 of the suite and no single module selects all of it.
That does NOT support the common claim that a dependency-based selector is
near-useless because it selects almost everything.

With pytest's conftest dependency included -- which is a real dependency, not
a modelling choice -- the mean rises to 0.6364 and 6 of the 12 source modules
select every test module, because conftest imports them. That is where the
claim comes from, and it holds only once the fixture coupling is counted.

The consequence for this product is the one worth stating: a selector built
from imports alone is not safe for a pytest suite. For regressselect.features
the import-only selection misses 0.5455 of the suite -- tests that depend
on it only through a fixture.
