FINDINGS, stated as measured
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. The dependency baseline has the highest APFD (0.836149) and, at matched
   cost, the lowest safety loss (0.172072 against 0.282883 for the
   learned selector). Baseline 1 wins this comparison outright.
2. The learned selector beats the history baseline on APFD (0.814752
   against 0.795608) and on safety loss at every budget, so the model is
   not a no-op; it is just not better than the safe baseline.
3. The dependency baseline's safety loss is 0.172072, not zero.
   Safe by construction means safe with respect to the declared import graph;
   the fixture history declares two channels the graph cannot see.
4. The learned selector's Brier score roughly doubles from the training slice
   to the held-out slice, so its probabilities are over-confident out of sample.
5. Import distance and reachability carry almost no weight in the fitted model
   (combined importance 0.0061); it keys on churn and co-failure.
   That is the mechanical reason it is less safe than the baseline that uses
   nothing but the dependency structure.
