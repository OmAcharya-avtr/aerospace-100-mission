implemented form against the series, by body and radius
==============================================================================
  The leading term of the expansion is first order in |r|/|r_b| and the
  next term is second order, so the leading term's RELATIVE error is first
  order: eps = |r|/|r_b|. The measured difference must therefore track eps,
  with a coefficient of order one that depends on the geometry.

  The check is two-sided and that matters. A difference much LARGER than
  eps would mean the implemented form is losing more precision than the
