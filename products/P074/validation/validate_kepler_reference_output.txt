step 3: the integrator against that reference
==============================================================================
  RK4 with only the central term, against the closed form at the same times.
  A fixed step is a poor fit to an eccentric orbit: the perigee passage is
  fast and the apogee arc is slow, so a step chosen per revolution is far
  too coarse at perigee. The declared tolerance therefore depends on the
  eccentricity, and the third case below is in the table to show the cost
  rather than to pass a loose check. It is why the population in
  fixtures/population.tle is capped near e = 0.05, which README.md states
  under Limitations.
