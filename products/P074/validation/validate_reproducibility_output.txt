claim 3: a subset reproduces the committed rows with the step pinned
==============================================================================
  rows compared                 : 24
  worst absolute difference     : 7.019874e-09
  worst relative difference     : 8.305428e-09

  This is NOT bit-exact, and the reason is the tabulated luni-solar
  ephemeris. A one-day run evaluates the trigonometric series on 1442
  epochs where the seven-day committed run used 10082, and numpy's
  vectorised transcendental loops are not required to give bit-identical
  results for arrays of different length -- the tail of a SIMD loop is
  handled differently from its body. A few thousand Runge-Kutta steps
  amplify that last-bit difference to the value above.
  [PASS] a pinned-step subset reproduces the committed rows to 1e-6 relative -- 8.305e-09
