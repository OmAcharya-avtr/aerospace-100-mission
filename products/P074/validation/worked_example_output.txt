step 4: the decision
==============================================================================
  The question: this element set is three days old and I need the
  position to better than 5.0 km. Is it still good enough?

  predictor                : conformal[analytic_power_altitude]
  tolerance                : 5.000 km
  median horizon           : 1.211 days
  conservative horizon     : 0.292 days (upper end of a 90% interval)
  interval coverage        : 0.8547 measured on held-out objects against 0.9000 nominal
                             the interval UNDER-COVERS; the conservative horizon above is optimistic by that much
  element set age          : 3.000 days
  predicted drift at age   : 15.598 km [2.627, 92.619]
  verdict                  : OUTSIDE

  Read it as follows. The median horizon is the optimistic answer and the
  one a point prediction gives: half the time the drift is already worse
  than it says. The conservative horizon is the one to use, and it is only
  as good as the measured coverage printed beside it.
