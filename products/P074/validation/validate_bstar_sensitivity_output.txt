  reference density rho_0       : 0.157 kg/(m^2 ER)

  what drag needs               : C_D A/m = 2 B* / rho_0

  Read this as: a factor of two error in the drag parameter moves the
  reported divergence by this fraction of itself. Where the fraction is
  small the curve is dominated by the difference in dynamical structure
  between SGP4 and the numerical model, which is the signal the product
  is about. Where it is large the curve is dominated by the drag
  assumption, and the number should be read as conditional on it.
