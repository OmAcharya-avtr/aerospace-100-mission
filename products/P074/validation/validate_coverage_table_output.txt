the conditional miscalibration conformal prediction does not promise
==============================================================================
  Split conformal prediction gives MARGINAL coverage at or above the
  nominal level for exchangeable data. It promises nothing about coverage
  conditional on a feature, and the tables above show it failing to
  deliver conditionally while delivering marginally. That is not a defect
  in the method; it is the method's stated scope, and a product that
  reported only the marginal number would be hiding it.
