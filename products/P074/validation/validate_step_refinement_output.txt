with drag on: the atmosphere is discontinuous, and it does not matter
==============================================================================
  The published atmosphere table is 28 independent exponential fits, so
  its band joins are discontinuous, which can cost a fixed-step method
  its formal order. The measured jumps are at most 0.14 per cent
  (validate_atmosphere_output.txt) and the orders below are
  indistinguishable from the drag-free orders above, so the mechanism is
  real and not material here.
