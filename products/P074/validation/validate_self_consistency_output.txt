what this baseline can and cannot see
==============================================================================
  Both sides are SGP4. Any error common to the theory cancels out of the
  comparison: the atmosphere model, the truncation of the Brouwer-Lyddane
  expansion, the absence of solar radiation pressure. What is left is the
  part of the error that came from the fit, which is a real and useful
  quantity and is not the whole error.
