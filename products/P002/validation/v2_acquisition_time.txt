REPORTED FINDING: passing the PER-DWELL probability to the analytic model
as if it were a per-crossing probability overestimates the acquisition time
by 38.0% at p_dwell = 0.9. The simulated beam covers the
target for ~2/step_fraction = 4 consecutive dwells, so the per-crossing
detection probability is 1 - (1-p_dwell)^4 = 0.99990, not 0.9. With that
correction the analytic model agrees with Monte Carlo to 0.56%.
