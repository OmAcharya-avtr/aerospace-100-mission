5. the strict threshold comparison of equation (6)
==============================================================================
The cloud indicator is a strict comparison of two rounded quantities. Two
builds that agree on the latent field and the threshold to within one unit
in the last place can still disagree on the indicator wherever the two sit
within an ulp of each other, and the number of such epochs grows with the
record. No test or check in this product asserts bit-exact equality of a
cloud indicator.

exactly those indicators whose margin |z - threshold| is inside delta, and
