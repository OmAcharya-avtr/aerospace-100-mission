2. the exact decomposition of equation (16)
==============================================================================
P_measured - P_naive = (P_conditional - P_naive) + (P_measured - P_conditional)

The identity is exact by construction and has no cross term, so its residual
is pure rounding. The first bracket is what assuming a STATIONARY climatology
costs and is signed: two stations whose seasonal cloud maxima fall six months
apart are jointly clear more often than their annual means suggest. The
second bracket is what assuming spatial INDEPENDENCE costs and is the
quantity the correlation models of this package predict.

3. the record's own sampling uncertainty, beside every figure
==============================================================================
The specification requires the climatology-uncertainty share propagated from
the record's own sampling uncertainty and reported beside every availability
figure. Equation (28) defines the share as

    share = s_boot^2 / (s_boot^2 + (P_model - P_hat)^2)

A share near 1 says the model and the record disagree by no more than the
record's own sampling noise, so the disagreement is not evidence of anything.
The corrected form subtracts s_boot^2 from the squared discrepancy and can
exceed 1, which is informative and is not clipped.
