2. the interval width against the block length
==============================================================================
A block bootstrap is consistent as L grows and L / T shrinks. A bootstrap
whose answer depends on its block length is reporting the block length, so
the dependence is measured rather than assumed away.

THE WIDTH DOES NOT PLATEAU, AND THIS IS A REPORTED NEGATIVE RESULT.

The standard error rises monotonically from 1.97e-03 at a block of one
epoch to 7.80e-03 at 512 epochs, a factor of 3.96, with no sign of settling
by 64 days. The reason is in the generator and not in the bootstrap: the
record carries a SEASONAL cycle as well as synoptic persistence, so the
joint-cloudy indicator is correlated at lags of months and no block short
enough to satisfy L / T -> 0 captures all of it. A seasonally
non-stationary series has no block length at which a block bootstrap is
exactly right.

The consequences, stated rather than left for a reader to find:

  - Every bootstrap interval this product quotes at a block of 64 epochs is
    a LOWER BOUND on the record's sampling uncertainty. A 512-epoch block
    gives a standard error 1.71 times larger on this network.
  - That direction is the safe one for this product's headline conclusion.
    The conclusion is that the learned model's margin falls INSIDE the
    bootstrap interval on the empirical reference; widening the interval can
    only strengthen it, never reverse it. The check below confirms that the
    conclusion survives the widest block measured.
  - It does NOT affect the headline overstatement figures themselves, which
    are differences of point estimates and carry their uncertainty beside
    them rather than inside them.

[PASS] the interval width rises monotonically with the block length and does NOT plateau, which is reported rather than hidden -- standard error 1.972045e-03 at L = 1 to 7.800017e-03 at L = 512, a factor of 3.9553, monotone at every step
[PASS] the declared block of 64 epochs is above the synoptic time scale and keeps L / T small -- 64 epochs = 8.0 days = 10.42 integral time scales, L / T = 0.002740
[PASS] control: the widest block measured would widen the interval, which is why the declared one is called a lower bound (must fire) -- FIRED; a 512-epoch block gives 7.800017e-03 against 4.571567e-03 at the declared 64, a factor of 1.7062

3. equation (22): the interval on a difference must be paired
==============================================================================
The quantity that decides this product's headline comparison is a
DIFFERENCE of two models' errors against the same reference. Resampling the
record changes the reference and therefore moves both errors together, so
the interval on the difference must come from paired replicates. Treating
the two as independent widens it and would hide a real difference.
