form, not a defect. As the inner quantity approaches 1 the derivative of
arcsin(sqrt(a)) diverges, so a relative perturbation eps in a becomes one of
order sqrt(eps) in the angle. The declared network's largest separation is
7299.5 km, well
inside half the circumference, so the regime is never entered in practice.
