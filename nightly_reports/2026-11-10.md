### The headline result

The independence assumption's overstatement of site-diversity availability,
measured on the declared anisotropic record (20 synthetic stations, 23 360
epochs, seed 20260002):

| network | n | P_independent | P_measured | ratio | nines lost |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| near-pair | 2 | 0.077335 ± 0.002064 | 0.171447 ± 0.005986 | 2.2169 | +0.3458 |
| far-pair | 2 | 0.121586 ± 0.002788 | 0.116139 ± 0.003971 | 0.9552 | −0.0199 |
| mixed-trio | 3 | 0.029901 ± 0.000910 | 0.062457 ± 0.002677 | 2.0888 | +0.3199 |
| cluster | 4 | 0.007256 ± 0.000264 | 0.103125 ± 0.004592 | **14.2124** | +1.1527 |
| two-groups | 6 | 0.000866 ± 0.000037 | 0.019906 ± 0.001472 | **22.9809** | +1.3614 |
| one-per-group | 5 | 0.007408 ± 0.000253 | 0.007149 ± 0.000714 | 0.9651 | −0.0154 |

Largest overstatement **22.9809**: an availability of 0.999134 claimed where
0.980094 is measured, **1.3614 nines that are not there**. The exact
decomposition closes with residual **0.00e+00** on all six networks.

**Two of the six networks have a ratio below 1** — their stations' seasonal
cloud maxima fall at different times of year, so the naive product *overstates*
their joint unavailability. The co-modulation term carries the sign and it is
reported rather than hidden.

### The finding of this session

**P082's learned model is closer to the empirical target and further from the
truth than either baseline.** Scored against the generator's own correlation
rather than the noisy empirical target, held-out SITES of the anisotropic
record:

| model | error against the truth |
|---|---|
| exponential | 0.043280 |
| per-regime | 0.043236 |
| learned | **0.063107** |

It is fitting the estimation noise in a target whose own rms error is 0.073751.
**Only a synthetic record with a known ground-truth correlation length can
expose this**, and it is the strongest argument in the product for the
synthetic record being synthetic.

### Weak and non-ideal results, reported rather than hidden

- The **uncertainty output is badly under-covered out of sample**: nominal 0.80,
  measured 0.8000 / 0.6147 / 0.4643 on train / held-out SITES / held-out TIME.
  A negative control asserts the under-coverage fires. 102 of 1520 quantile
  crossings are counted, not repaired.
- The **block bootstrap does not plateau**: the standard error rises
  monotonically by a factor of **3.9553** from a 1-epoch to a 512-epoch block,
  because the record carries a seasonal cycle as well as synoptic persistence.
  Every interval quoted at the declared 64 epochs is a **lower bound**. That
  direction only strengthens "the margin is inside the interval".
- **Held-out TIME is not a test of temporal extrapolation.** No feature encodes
  the time half, so a held-out-TIME row receives the same prediction as its
  training twin and only the target differs. Stated in the model card, the
  README and the validation output so the result cannot be overstated.
- The **Bonferroni bracket is vacuous for 3 of 6 networks**; the Fréchet lower
  bound is informative for only **8 of 190 pairs**. Both counts are printed
  rather than a pass being claimed on the full set.
- **Equation (13) disagrees with a block bootstrap on 20 of 20 stations** by
  more than 5 per cent.
- One declared leak: the marginal climatology is estimated once over the whole
  record and used in both halves — identically for every model compared.

### The data is synthetic and the product says so unambiguously

No real site climatology is used. The record is an exponentially-correlated
Gaussian field thresholded to a binary cloud indicator, with seasonal and
diurnal modulation, generated by a committed fixed-seed script so the true
correlation length is known. `DATASET_CARD.md` and the README state this and
what it means for the transferability of every number. **This is a limitation
and it is also what makes the session's sharpest finding measurable at all.**
