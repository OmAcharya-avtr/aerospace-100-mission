### The L4 counting question, re-audited from the registry rather than from last night's report

This is tonight's only substantive finding and it is a correction to the
*reports*, not to the repository.

**What last night's report said.** That achieved Level 4 is 4 of 5 (P091-P094),
and that "P095 occupies an L4 target slot it has not achieved and is labelled
`Level 3, hardware-pending`".

**What the registry actually contains.** A `yaml.safe_load` parse finds exactly
one entry carrying a `validation_label` field, and it is **P079 TimeTransfer**,
`validation_label: "Level 3, hardware-pending"` at `validation_level: 3` -
label and level in agreement, counted as L3, internally consistent. **P095
carries no `validation_label` field at all.** Read only that far, P095 looks
like an unmeasured product recorded as a bare `validation_level: 4`, which
would be an overstated validation claim and a defect this mission forbids.

**It is not a defect, and the registry says so at length.** P095 carries:

- `validation_level_achieved: 3`, and
- a `validation_level_note` stating that the `validation_level` field above it
  is the slot's TARGET and not an achieved level, that the product is labelled
  "Level 3, hardware-pending" in its own README, badge and `VALIDATION.md`,
  that no figure in it was measured on a Jetson Orin Nano, and that the
  one-line `quota_report.py` change was **deliberately not made** because
  editing the mission's measurement of record from an unattended session is the
  class of act the mission forbids.

P095's own `validation/VALIDATION.md` opens with "**Validation level: Level 3,
hardware-pending**", reports 0 figures measured on the declared target and 14
of 14 channels `verified_on_hardware: False`, and its only Orin fixture is
named `orin_nano_idle_simulated_trace.json`. The product understates rather
than overstates itself.

P091-P094 each carry `validation_level_achieved: 4` with a dated, scoped note
naming the board, the campaign size and the limits of generality - P091 17 runs
/ 4992 samples, P092 190662 jobs on 2026-11-13, P093 20 runs / 27.05 minutes on
2026-11-14, P094 810,000 frames / 734.03 s on 2026-11-15, each on an NVIDIA
Jetson Orin Nano Engineering Reference Developer Kit Super at MAXN_SUPER. Each
note ends "NEEDS THE OWNER to ratify or reject".

**Therefore:** achieved L4 is **4 of 5**; occupied L4 target slots are **5 of
5**; `quota_report.py` counts the latter and prints 5/5 correctly for what it
measures. **Nothing was edited.** Tonight's session came close to adding a
`validation_label` to P095 on the strength of last night's wording and stopped
on reading the registry itself. The owner's decision is unchanged and is
recorded in four places in `products.yaml` already: either teach
`quota_report.py` to prefer `validation_level_achieved`, or accept the
target-slot reading. **An unattended session must not make that edit, and did
not.**

## Lessons recorded for the next session

1. **READ THE REGISTRY, NOT LAST NIGHT'S DESCRIPTION OF IT.** This session was
   one edit away from adding a `validation_label` to P095 because the previous
   report said P095 was "labelled Level 3, hardware-pending" and a parse showed
   no such field. The field belongs to P079. P095 instead carries
   `validation_level_achieved` and a long explanatory note that answers the
   question completely. A report summarising a registry is not the registry,
   and the gap between them looks exactly like a defect.
2. **AN APPARENT OVERSTATEMENT MAY BE DOCUMENTED ELSEWHERE IN THE SAME
   ENTRY.** `validation_level: 4` with no caveat field read as an unmeasured L4
   claim. Two fields further down the same entry said the opposite, in detail.
   Read the whole entry and the product's own `VALIDATION.md` before recording
   a validation defect.
3. **A PREVIOUS SESSION'S REFUSAL TO EDIT IS EVIDENCE, NOT AN OMISSION.** P095's
   note explains why the one-line `quota_report.py` fix was not made. That
   reasoning was correct and tonight's session deferred to it rather than
   relitigating it unattended.
4. **DERIVE THE WORK LIST ON THE MACHINE THAT WILL DO THE WORK.** The
   contributor sweep's slug list was generated on the Mac from the Mac's own
   `products.yaml` with `grep` and `sed`, so no payload crossed the bridge and
   the 2026-11-24 and 2026-11-25 carry-corruption class was avoided entirely
   rather than checksummed around. Applies the "never retype a payload" lesson
   by removing the payload.
5. **CHUNK A 100-CALL SWEEP INTO SYNCHRONOUS BATCHES INSTEAD OF BACKGROUNDING
   IT.** Four `osascript` calls of 25 `gh api` calls each returned inside the
   timeout with no lock file, no `__DONE__` marker and no silent-zero-iteration
   risk. Simpler than the 2026-11-25 background-sweep machinery and it cannot
   report completion without having done the work.
6. **DO NOT RETRY A 403 THAT TELLS YOU NOT TO RETRY IT.** The container API
   refusal names its own fix and states that a scheduled run cannot apply it.
   One probe, then move the work to the Mac and name the missing repositories
   in the report.
