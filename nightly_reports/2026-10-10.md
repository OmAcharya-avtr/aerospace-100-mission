## FINDING OF THE NIGHT — last night's four-agent fan-out produced nothing retained

This is the most important thing in this report and it is a repeat, in a new
form, of the failure mode this task version exists to prevent.

The 2026-10-09 session pushed its Rule Zero report (`bfecc75`) and its
pre-fan-out checkpoint (`94625a0`), then dispatched four build agents in one
block for P056 CalibAudit, P057 ConformalBand, P058 TelemDrift and P060
TraceAudit. `94625a0` is still the tip of `origin/main`. Measured against the
committed tree this morning:

- `products/` contains P001–P055 and P059. **There is no `products/P056`,
  `products/P057`, `products/P058` or `products/P060`.**
- `products.yaml` still records all four as `SPECIFYING`.
- No commit after `94625a0` exists on `origin/main` or in the Mac working copy.

So four agents were dispatched and **none of their output was ever committed or
pushed**. On the measured Batch 06 figures (five concurrent agents averaged
roughly 400k tokens each) that is on the order of 1.5–2M agent tokens spent and
discarded, plus the coordinating session's own usage.

The distinction from the v4 failure matters. v4 could not publish at all — it
had no device. Last night **could** publish, proved it could with a write probe,
pushed two commits, and still lost the build, because the session ended between
dispatch and harvest. **Phase −1 is necessary and is not sufficient.** The
remaining gap is that a ~1h45m fan-out does not fit reliably inside the session
window that remains after dispatch, and an unharvested agent is an unpushed
agent.

Mitigation applied tonight, recorded so it can be judged rather than assumed:
the durable, cheap work that was outstanding is done and pushed FIRST, the
owner escalation goes out as a notification early rather than at the end, and
agent output is harvested and pushed incrementally as each agent returns
instead of in one block at the end.

## The two owner decisions — fourth and third escalation

Batch 06 now has **five finished, gate-PASS products held unpublished for a
third consecutive night**: P051 SimplexGuard, P052 FalsifyLoop, P054 RareVerify,
P055 AssureGraph, P059 InvariantSet. Nothing about them is defective. They are
held by a conflict in the mission's own rules.

**1. P053 exit-code convention (fourth escalation).**
`products/P053/validation/validate_thresholds.py` exits **1 by design**. It
publishes two failing variance-CUSUM threshold-transfer checks — bias −8.6 % at
T=200 and +13.5 % at T=1000 against a declared 8.8 % tolerance — **instead of
widening the tolerance until they pass**. That is ADR-011's honest-negative
policy working exactly as written. `scripts/release_gate.py` check 6 requires
every `validation/*.py` to exit 0. The gate therefore punishes the single
behaviour the mission most wants, and one product's honesty blocks five
siblings.

Not resolved here, deliberately, for the same reason as the two previous
sessions: flipping that exit code would clear the gate tonight without
addressing the gate's concern, and would settle permanently and unilaterally a
convention question already referred to the owner. An unattended session with
no human input is the wrong place to overturn that referral. **The gate was not
edited, no threshold loosened, no failing check hidden, no check skipped.**

The decision is narrow and is the owner's: should a validation script be allowed
to signal "this check failed and the failure is the finding" with a non-zero
exit and still pass the gate — for example by having the gate read a declared
`expected_exit` or an `honest_negative: true` marker — or should ADR-011
negatives be reported through a mechanism that exits 0?

**2. Batch atomicity (third escalation).**
If a batch may publish partially, five repositories go out with no further
building. The standing rule is that gate exit 1 publishes nothing from the batch
and the parts that passed are not pushed, so **nothing was published tonight
either**. Running the gate over a hand-picked subset to obtain exit 0 is
"publish the parts that passed" with extra steps and was not done.
