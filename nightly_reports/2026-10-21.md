Measured results worth naming:

- Foster–Estes, Chan and Alfano implemented independently and scored against 2-D
  quadrature and 3-D Monte Carlo. Against the exact non-central chi-square CDF
  from `scipy.stats.ncx2`: Foster worst relative error **6.66e-16**, Chan at its
  recommended order **1.11e-15**, quadrature **5.55e-16**.
- Monte Carlo interval coverage **0.960** (288/300 seeds) against nominal 0.95,

  raised by the library on both dilution-branch cases and on neither other.
- Rectilinear-motion assumption deliberately violated: 1 per cent error at
  relative speed **0.81 m/s** for a declared 200 m LEO encounter, so the 2-D
  formulation is excellent for a fast conjunction and unusable below ~1 m/s.

**Two results contradict the specification's own predictions and are published
as measured, not retuned away:**

1. A **one-line closed form using the same features and no training step beats
   the learned random forest on two of three populations** (recall 1.00000
   against 0.98840 and 0.99784 at ~1 per cent FPR). The forest leads only on the
   population it was trained on, by +0.01551. The spec predicted a Mahalanobis
   threshold would match the model; it does not — it is the *worst* analytic
   screen at 0.03436 — and the measured reason is that Mahalanobis is invariant
   to `R/sigma_eff`, which spans 3.92 decades on that population and 0.26 on the
   one where the same screen recovers to 0.83720. The prediction holds under a
   condition the spec did not state.
2. **One validation check FAILS and ships as failed.** The learned
   probabilities are miscalibrated: worst reliability-bin gap **0.272** against
   a declared 0.15 tolerance. Not repaired, because recall at fixed FPR depends
   only on score ordering and is invariant under any monotone recalibration — a
   Platt or isotonic fit would move the Brier score and not move a single recall
   number. Stated in README, VALIDATION.md and MODEL_CARD.md as: *the predicted
   probability is usable as a ranking and is not usable as a probability.*

**Six defects were found by Hypothesis and none by the hand-written tests, and
four of the six are defects in the published equations as literally
transcribed**: an absolute quadrature tolerance that destroyed scale invariance
(5.3 per cent disagreement between a geometry and the same geometry scaled by
19, both values wrong); the published `erf` chord mass cancelling to exactly 0.0
where the answer is 8.77e-111; Chan's finite exponential sum losing 12
significant figures to cancellation; and Chan's recommended series order of 3
being 15 per cent wrong at M=12. All six are pinned as named regression tests
with literal inputs.

caught only by the reference implementation, not by any test.** Unscaled
capacity rows — coefficients around 1e9 against an objective around 1e1 — made
HiGHS return `status == 0` together with an objective **below the true optimum

row is now divided by its window capacity.
