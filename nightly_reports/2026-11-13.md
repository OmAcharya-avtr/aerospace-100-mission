## Three campaigns ran on the board and two were discarded

**Campaign 1 (board 23:14:39, ran 36 minutes to completion, discarded).** All ten
job traces were written and every one is a valid measurement. **Not one monitor
file was.** The 1 Hz monitor is launched as a subprocess of the campaign script,
and the launch omitted the `--out` argument that the script's own `argparse`
declares required, so every monitor process exited 2 before writing a row, with
its stderr sent to `DEVNULL`. The campaign reported success. The defect was
visible only as ten absent `mon_*.csv` files, and it would have removed every
contention descriptor — the entire learned half of the product — while leaving
the headline looking healthy.

Campaign 1 **also** mis-sized the payload. It calibrated to 0.4499 of the
deadline from a 1.2 s probe taken immediately after the board went idle, when
`schedutil` had already begun dropping the clock. The sustained median across its
ten runs came out near 0.19 of the deadline — a factor of 2.3 — so the task
loaded one core to about 19 per cent, **six of ten runs recorded zero misses**,
and the miss-rate curve was not monotone (0.0015 at four stressors, 0.0345 at
five, 0.0005 at six). A tail product calibrated that way measures almost nothing.

**Campaign 2 (02:59:19, killed after 3 minutes).** Monitor fixed, same
calibration defect, killed. **Killing it left five orphaned CPU stressors running
at about 97 per cent each**, found by listing the board's processes and killed by
PID before campaign 3 started. A campaign launched on top of them would have
measured a board with five uncontrolled busy threads and called the result a
contention level.

**Campaign 3 (03:02:08, 37 minutes, published).** Monitor launch fixed; payload
sized by a **sustained** calibration — a 30 s run with the first third discarded
as governor ramp — which reached 0.7315 of the deadline on its first attempt with
all six cores already at 1728000 kHz. Every run carries its monitor file. The
miss-rate curve is monotone from two stressors upward and only `mix_mem2`
recorded zero misses.

**A campaign that completes is not a campaign that measured.** Both defects
produced a clean exit status, a full log and a plausible set of files.

## Defects found and fixed this session

1. **A check that could not fail.** `validate_tree_hygiene.py` section 2 ran
   `git ls-files` with its working directory set to the product root, which
   prints paths **relative to that directory**, and then filtered them for the
   prefix `products/P092/`, which they never carry. The filtered list was always
   empty, so the tracked-artifact check was vacuous — not only before the
   coordinator commits, which the script's own note excused, but after. Rewritten
   to `--full-name` and **verified in both directions**: 77 tracked files PASS, a
   planted tracked `.pyc` FAILS with the offending path named. Sixth occurrence
   of this class in the mission.
2. **A descriptor the specification does not license was the model's top
   feature.** `task_pct`, the task's own CPU occupancy, carried importance
   0.654312. The headline was neither withdrawn nor retuned; a new script,
   `validate_descriptor_leak.py`, refits the same forest with the same seed and
   splits on two narrower sets. Removing `task_pct` and `load1` costs 1.1354×
   grouped and 1.2619× on time, and the restricted figure still beats both
   baselines. Published as an upper bound, labelled as one.
3. **Two column-width collisions in committed output.** `8.897912e-08none` and
   `0.000000e+007.939543e-165` — fields exactly as wide as their values print
   with no padding. Widened and re-run.
4. **The campaign's own log was silently excluded from the product.** The mission
   root `.gitignore` has a bare `*.log`, which matches at any depth. Recovered
   with a negation in the product `.gitignore`. **Any product shipping a `.log`
   has the same problem and will not be told.**
5. **Three totals written from arithmetic rather than measurement** were caught by
   measuring: the campaign's releases and misses, which the coordinator first
   wrote as 210 010 and 8756 against a measured 195 010 and 8556.
