## The decision this session did NOT make

Batch 06 has been built-but-unpublished for two nights. Five products
(P051, P052, P054, P055, P059) are gate-PASS. One sibling, **P053
TwinInvalidate**, blocks the batch, and the reason is a genuine conflict in the
mission's own rules, not a defect in P053:

- `products/P053/validation/validate_thresholds.py` exits **1 by design**,
  because it publishes two failing variance-CUSUM threshold-transfer checks
  (bias −8.6 % at T=200, +13.5 % at T=1000 against a declared 8.8 % tolerance)
  **rather than widening the tolerance to pass**. That is ADR-011 working
  exactly as intended.
- `release_gate.py` check 6 requires every `validation/*.py` to exit 0.

So the gate currently punishes the behaviour the mission most wants. Two
sessions have now escalated this. **This session did not resolve it either, and
deliberately so**, on two counts:

1. **It did not publish the five that pass.** The standing rule is explicit —
   exit 1 publishes nothing from the batch and the parts that passed are not
   pushed. Whether a batch is atomic for publication is one of the open owner
   decisions. Running the gate over a hand-picked subset to obtain exit 0 would
   be "publish the parts that passed" with extra steps, so it was not done.
2. **It did not change P053's exit convention.** Flipping that script to exit 0
   would make the gate pass tonight without the gate's concern being addressed,
   and it would settle, unilaterally and permanently, a convention question that
   the previous session explicitly referred to the owner. An unattended session
   with no human input is the wrong place to overturn that referral.

What changed tonight is the **channel**: the two decisions had only ever been
written into reports and a checkpoint. They now go to the owner as a push
notification, because three consecutive nights blocked on an unread paragraph is
a reporting failure, not a build failure.
