## What P095 is, and the finding

The hardware abstraction layer the four remaining Level 4 products sit on: one
interface over the board's clock, die-temperature, rail-power and
performance-counter sources; three backends behind it (live, recorded,
simulated); declared simulation and dry-run modes; deployment and recovery
procedures; and the benchmark harness itself. Provenance is the type system
rather than a logging feature — every reading carries `LIVE`, `RECORDED` or
`SIMULATED`, provenance propagates through every derivation as a lattice meet,
and a result can be labelled `MEASURED` only if its propagated provenance is
`LIVE` throughout.

### THE FINDING: two laundering routes worked before they were closed

The adversarial check tried fifteen routes to get a simulated reading into a
result labelled measured. Thirteen were refused by the first design. **Two were
not.** Both are recorded rather than quietly fixed.

**1. `dataclasses.replace(reading, provenance=LIVE)`** — public API on every
dataclass. It re-ran `__post_init__`, which **recomputed the tamper seal**, so
the laundered reading was *validly sealed*, carried the simulated reading's own
channel and value, claimed `LIVE`, and `certify()` granted it `MEASURED`. One
line, no private state touched. The general statement worth keeping is this:
**a seal recomputed by the mechanism being abused is not a seal.** Closed by
making `Reading`'s first field an `InitVar` holding a module-private minting
token, so `dataclasses.replace` and `copy.replace` both raise and
`boardprobe.readings.mint()` is the single minting entry point.

**2. Rebinding `SimulatedSource.backend_provenance`**, an ordinary class
attribute read through `type(self)`. Closed by capturing the three backends'
declared provenances in a module-private registry at import.

Both are pinned as named regression tests with literal inputs. Three residual
holes are stated in the product rather than omitted: `mint()` lets its caller
choose a provenance (a false claim at the source, which no type system can
stop, now narrowed to one named auditable function attributable to a
`source_id`); a `PlatformAttestation` can be hand-built with
`matches_target=True`; and same-process code can reach module-private state.
The guarantee is that **a provenance once claimed cannot be strengthened,
laundered through a derivation, or lost** — not that a false claim cannot be
made at the source.

Final state: **15 routes attempted, 15 refused, 0 succeeded**, plus **3
positive controls** granted on inputs that admit them, because a check that
only observes refusals cannot distinguish a working invariant from a function
that always raises.
