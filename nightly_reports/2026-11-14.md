## The board's rail-power precondition, which could have cancelled the product

The 2026-11-13 next_action required this check before any agent was dispatched,
because without a rail measurement P093's headline cannot exist and the product
would have needed rescoping. **Rail power is available by two independent
routes and the precondition is discharged:**

- `tegrastats` reports `VDD_IN`, `VDD_CPU_GPU_CV`, `VDD_SOC` in mW;
- an **INA3221** at `/sys/class/hwmon/hwmon1` (i2c `1-0040`) exposes per-channel
  bus voltage and shunt current in sysfs, which is what the campaign used.

Two instrument properties were measured before the campaign was designed and
both are reported with every figure: the current register **quantises at 8 mA**,
a **40 mW least-significant bit** at 5 V (confirmed from the traces at
39.883 mW), and the part **averages 512 conversions in hardware**
(`update_interval` 1, `samples` 512), so the energy integral is sound while the
*shape* of the wake transient is bandwidth-limited.
