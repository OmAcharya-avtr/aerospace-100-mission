# Risk Register

Reviewed 2026-08-29 during orchestration recovery.

| ID | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-01 | 100 products in 4 weeks exceeds real session throughput | Certain (occurred) | High | Re-baselined: one batch per productive session, ~9–12 sessions for the remaining 85 products. Quality gates take precedence over the original calendar. | OPEN — schedule rebaselined |
| R-02 | GitHub PAT pasted in chat (violates §8); account password also exposed 2026-08-29 | Certain (occurred) | **High** | Both credentials treated as compromised. Owner rotating both. Replacement read only from secure credential storage or `GITHUB_TOKEN`, never from a repository file. ADR-014. | **REOPENED — ACTION: OWNER.** Previously closed as accepted risk on 2026-08-01; that acceptance is withdrawn. |
| R-03 | Automated environment blocked from creating GitHub repos | Occurred, then **mis-generalized** | Medium | Root cause corrected: the block is per-environment authorization, not a mission-wide limit. Capability test required before any assumption. ADR-004. | **CLOSED 2026-08-29** — superseded by R-13 |
| R-04 | Nightly sessions cannot reach the token if the Mac is asleep or the desktop app is closed | Certain (occurred) | **High** | Root cause of 22 lost days. Removed: mission state now recovers from GitHub, not from a bundle on the Mac. ADR-007. | **CLOSED 2026-08-29** |
| R-05 | Cloud container is ephemeral; unpushed work is lost on reclaim | Medium | High | Checkpoint after every phase to `tracking/SESSION_CHECKPOINT.json`; commit locally at each checkpoint; publish at session end subject to the approval gate. ADR-013. | OPEN — mitigated |
| R-06 | Overstated aerospace claims damage credibility | Low | High | Status vocabulary enforced; validation evidence required; no flight-safety or certification language, ever. | OPEN |
| R-07 | Name conflicts with existing packages | Medium | Low | PyPI/npm/GitHub conflict check per product before use. P002 conflict found and resolved (TrackForge → TrackBench). | OPEN — process working |
| R-08 | AI products lacking meaningful baselines fail §11 | Medium | Medium | Baseline-first development: classical or analytic baseline implemented and benchmarked before the ML model. Nine documented cases where the baseline wins are retained as evidence. ADR-011. | OPEN — process working |
| R-09 | Large artifacts bloat repositories | Medium | Low | `.gitignore` excludes checkpoints and datasets; regeneration scripts committed instead. | OPEN |
| R-10 | 2-core build container limits training scale | High | Medium | Small models, synthetic datasets, budgeted training runs; compute limits disclosed in every model card. PyTorch unavailable; scikit-learn/NumPy only. ADR-005. | OPEN — accepted |
| R-11 | **Scheduled automation fails silently** | Certain (occurred) | **High** | The nightly task fired once (2026-08-07), aborted in 57 s, and its schedule stalled with `next_run_at` frozen at 2026-08-08. Nobody noticed for 21 days. Mitigation: completion notifications enabled; every run writes a report to `nightly_reports/` whether or not it builds; a run that produces no commit still reports why. | OPEN — monitor |
| R-12 | **An environment-local limitation is written down as a mission-wide fact** | Occurred | **High** | This is what cost the mission its publication path for three weeks. Mitigation: no limitation may be recorded without naming the environment and the date tested; capability tests are cheap and must be re-run rather than cited from memory. ADR-004. | OPEN — process change made |
| R-13 | Build session (Environment A) is not authorized to push, so build and publish are split across two environments | Medium | Medium | Works today via the owner's local environment. Permanent fix: attach the four mission repositories as sources to the build session, bringing publication inside Environment A. | OPEN — action available |
| R-14 | **The implementing model validates its own numerical claims** | Medium | High | Separation enforced: the coordinating session or a distinct reviewing agent re-runs every test suite and re-derives every critical validation number. Build-agent self-reports are never entered into a tracker unverified. ADR-008. | OPEN — process change made |
| R-15 | Class and validation-level quotas drift toward whatever is easiest to build | **Occurred** | Medium | Compact products are at 10 of 50 while flagship sits at 2 of 20 and Level 4 at 0 of 5. Mitigation: totals computed from `products.yaml`, not asserted; every batch spec states the quota gap it closes. Level 4 must start by Batch 05. ADR-009. | OPEN — correcting in Batch 02 |
